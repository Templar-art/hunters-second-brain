# ZED Style (staging piece-list)

**⚠️ Superseded 2026-09-10.** Zed sent through a new reference set (Zillow-style listing photos) and confirmed it's the new direction, replacing the white/cream spec below. New style: **warm earthy neutral — taupe/olive/stone, bouclé, fluted timber, timber floors, black allowed.** Old white/cream/gold/no-timber spec kept below for the record; do not use it for new jobs.

This page is the single source for the piece-list. Job files (`projects/akmal/JOB.md`, `projects/zed-full-house/JOB.md`) should reference it rather than restate it.

## Piece-list (current, 2026-09-10)

**Palette** — warm taupe / olive / stone neutrals, not cool sage-blue. Black is now allowed (dining chair legs, side tables, marble). Timber is now allowed (herringbone oak floors, fluted/reeded timber joinery, walnut-tone bedsides) — this reverses the old "no timber" rule.

**Living** — deep emerald-green tubular/channel-tufted velvet modular sofa; low black/wood-mix coffee table stack; textured neutral (not shag) area rug; one bouclé rounded accent chair (Pacific-style); brass/bronze table lamp; warm-toned landscape art (not cool abstract).

**Dining** — light-wood table with black legs/base; bouclé or fabric dining chairs on black metal legs.

**Bedroom** — upholstered bed in warm neutral fabric, headboard same tone; layered white/beige/taupe linen with an olive-green accent cushion or two, folded taupe/tan throw; bedsides can be walnut-timber (not just white-gloss) or white with brass hardware — mixed left/right is fine; warm mushroom-style or dome table lamps in brass/bronze; bouclé rounded chair or bench (Pacific-style) at the foot; textured neutral rug under the bed; warm-toned landscape/abstract art (earth tones, not blue-green).

**Kitchen** — stone/travertine-look benchtop and splashback; fluted/reeded cabinet fronts in warm cream/taupe; curved island end. (Cabinetry itself is a fixture — preserve as shot; palette note is for any added styling/props only.)

**Outdoor / patio** — still weatherproof only, but palette shifts warm-neutral (rope/cord chairs, warm-grey cushions, not strictly white/cream) — confirm exact patio set with Zed before locking; nothing in the reference set shown yet contradicts the "weatherproof, in-palette, nothing new built" rule.

## Rules that still ride with it
- Plants rotate between rooms — never the same plant twice in one listing.
- Secondary bedrooms styled a tier below the primary.
- Fixtures preserved everywhere: floors, windows and the view, cabinetry, stone, appliances, curtains, tapware, built-ins.
- Round dining table still the fallback shape wherever end-chair risk applies (see [[staging-rules]]) — the new ref set didn't show a round table, so keep this rule live until told otherwise.

Full standing rules: [[staging-rules]]. Pipeline routing and models: [[higgsfield-pipelines]].

## Piece-list (superseded 2026-09-10 — white/cream era)

The house style 10x used for full-restyle virtual staging: white / cream modern luxury. **No black, no timber furniture.**

**Dining** — white stone/concrete-top table (round or rectangular) on a substantial matte-white pedestal or block base; 4 cream upholstered dining chairs with slightly curved backs and armrests, evenly spaced; one low matte-cream ceramic bowl as the centrepiece. Approved 2026-08-24, replacing the earlier marble-and-gold version.

**Living** — cream bouclé L-sectional; white marble + brushed-gold rectangular coffee table; cream shag rug wider than the sofa's front legs; one ivory accent chair; gold-stem floor lamp; fiddle-leaf fig in a white ceramic pot.

**Bedroom** — white-gloss bed with a cream bouclé headboard, layered white and cream linen, no more than three accent cushions, folded cream throw; white-gloss bedsides styled differently left and right; gold-stem lamps; kentia palm in white ceramic; cream shag rug clear of the window and door.

**Outdoor / patio** — rectangular white concrete table with 4 cream chairs on the long sides only. Indoor velvet and marble pieces never go outside.
