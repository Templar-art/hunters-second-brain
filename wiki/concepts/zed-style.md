# ZED Style (staging piece-list)

The house style 10x uses for full-restyle virtual staging: white / cream modern luxury. **No black, no timber furniture.** Every frame in a listing draws from the same set, so the rooms read as one home rather than one prompt each.

This page is the single source for the piece-list. Job files (`projects/akmal/JOB.md`, `projects/zed-full-house/JOB.md`) should reference it rather than restate it.

## Piece-list

**Dining** — white stone/concrete-top table (round or rectangular) on a substantial matte-white pedestal or block base; 4 cream upholstered dining chairs with slightly curved backs and armrests, evenly spaced; one low matte-cream ceramic bowl as the centrepiece. This exact table+chair design is the locked default — approved 2026-08-24, replacing the earlier marble-and-gold version. Round table stays the default shape where end-chair risk applies (see [[staging-rules]]).

**Living** — cream bouclé L-sectional; white marble + brushed-gold rectangular coffee table; cream shag rug wider than the sofa's front legs; one ivory accent chair; gold-stem floor lamp; fiddle-leaf fig in a white ceramic pot.

**Bedroom** — white-gloss bed with a cream bouclé headboard, layered white and cream linen, no more than three accent cushions, folded cream throw; white-gloss bedsides styled differently left and right; gold-stem lamps; kentia palm in white ceramic; cream shag rug clear of the window and door.

**Outdoor / patio** — rectangular white concrete table with 4 cream chairs on the long sides only. Indoor velvet and marble pieces never go outside.

## Rules that ride with it

- Plants rotate between rooms — fig in living, kentia in the bedroom, and so on. Never the same plant twice in one listing.
- Secondary bedrooms are styled a tier below the primary.
- Fixtures are preserved everywhere: floors, windows and the view, cabinetry, stone, appliances, curtains, tapware, built-ins.
