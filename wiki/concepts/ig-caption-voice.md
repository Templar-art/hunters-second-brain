# Instagram Caption Voice

The core discipline: **every account gets captions in its OWN feed voice.** Test before shipping: "would this caption sound at home on @10xvisuals.co?" — if yes, it's wrong for a client.

## @10xvisuals.co — two modes (auto-memory `feedback-instagram-caption-voice`)
- **Organic showcase:** understated brag, 👀, no hashtags/CTA, no slang.
- **Ad:** pitch + DM CTA + hashtags; approved formula in auto-memory `reference-ig-ad-checklist` (verify handles; request 9:16 for Reels placement).

## Client voices (studied, locked)
- **Rob / [[coast-life-constructions|CLC]]:** short plain sentences, understated, concrete ("Walls and ceilings lined, coming together nicely now"). Clever editorial aphorisms = rejected.
- **Jason Ward / [[bespoke-estate-agents|Bespoke]]:** "Welcome to [address]…" openers, listing prose, dash-separated spec line, contact block, celebratory SOLD register — full study on the client page. Never write media/shoot talk into client captions.

## Universal caption rules
- No em dashes (10x + Bespoke explicit rule).
- Verified claims only; no volume numbers public ([[brand-system]]).
