## Standing design rules (all 10x graphics)
- **No dashes** in copy. Nothing moves. Colour discipline, photo-first, grid rhythm (auto-memory `feedback-social-design`).
- **Never dim/cover the photo's subject with type**; type in bottom gradient only.
- **Premium law** (from reference research, [[10x-ig-repositioning]]): the less graphic design on the grid, the more premium it reads — type on footage, never tiles.
- Every deck/carousel shares **one premium finish** — unify mixed photos with a subtle consistent grade.
- Volume numbers never public; no founder-always-on-site claims (auto-memory `feedback-client-claims-and-photo-cover`).
