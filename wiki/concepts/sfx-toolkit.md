# SFX toolkit

Four free sound tools were installed on 5 Nov 2026. They sit alongside [[auto-sound-design]], which keeps song cuts and the FCP-ready ambience stems.

| Tool | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Real SFX** (Claude skill, Clipwing) | Places real recordings from Hunter's libraries on exact frames of an MP4. Returns a new MP4, an SFX-only WAV, a cue report and a self-critique. | Tasteful passes in the [[desire-the-pilot-reference]] style: sparse whooshes on motion cuts and reveals, plus a sub hit on the drop. |
| **Earshot** (MCP, local CPU) | Describes, matches and ranks sounds in words. Listens through a whole film (timeline, sheet). Tells real recordings from synthetic ones. | Vetting library sounds before use, for example catching voices or cicadas in birds-only beds, and checking a finished mix. |
| **SFXMint** (MCP) | Searches and downloads CC0 sound effects. No key or credit needed. | Filling gaps in the library. |
| **Openverse** (MCP) | Searches Creative Commons audio. | A second source. Check every licence: CC-BY needs a credit, and NC is never OK for client work. |

Real SFX libraries: the main one is `~/Music/SFX Library` (1,634 sounds, mostly Ocular). The fallbacks are the Ultimate Creator pack, RN Sound Effects and ReelAudio, for 2,120 indexed sounds in total. The skill comes with 78 CC0 UI sounds, which a real-estate edit rarely needs.

Not yet run on a real edit. The first real test is waiting on Hunter's video cut to [[auto-sound-design|ZOYA "Pulled Under"]].
