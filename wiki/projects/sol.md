# SOL

SŌL — 14 Binda Place, Sorrento QLD. A 10x Visuals property shoot that became one of the five case studies on [[10x-website]], and the page that taught the site build two separate lessons.

## What exists

Hunter's own footage, cut as a video hero (transcoded from 4K/120–180MB down to ~12MB 1080p, re-edited once), plus stills. The case-study page is `/sol` on `10xvisuals.com.au`, scaffolded from `scripts/build_case_studies.py` in [projects/10x-site/](../../projects/10x-site/). Posted to [[10x-ig-repositioning|@10xvisuals_]] Sep 2025 — the "You don't walk past a place like this without noticing" clip, its best-performing post of that period at 90 likes.

## What it taught us

**A hero is chosen by where the dark is, not by how good the photo is.** The SOL drone frame is the stronger photograph and still made the worse cover: its lower third is bright white roof and pale concrete, exactly where the headline sits. Stonehaven replaced it on 30 Jul because its foreground lawn and paving give ivory type a real bed. The full version of this rule lives on [[10x-website]].

**Blocked is a legitimate report; invented imagery is not.** The SOL image sequence (ticket ENG-02) stayed blocked while `raw/`, an external SSD, Desktop, Downloads and the live WP page were all checked for usable frames. Nothing was found, and the correct outcome was to say so rather than scaffold placeholder pictures. Hunter's call closed it a different way — **SOL ships with the video instead** — and the other five case studies, which turned out to be in the identical single-photo situation, were built to the same honest standard.
