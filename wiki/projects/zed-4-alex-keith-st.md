## What it taught us
- Shrink sofas to sell space: a big sectional made the rumpus feel small.
- Where window bottoms were never photographed, keep a sofa back covering the sills, or the model invents short sills.
- Waist-height half-walls get filled in unless named and locked.
- Straight-on angles beat obliques for outdoor furniture geometry.
