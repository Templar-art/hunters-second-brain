# Final Cut Pro plugin research

The goal is to work out which Final Cut Pro plugins and companion apps would make 10x edits faster or better, without paying for anything FCP already does or that's already installed. The research is being done in Perplexity; the prompts are in [projects/fcp-plugin-research/perplexity-prompts.md](../../projects/fcp-plugin-research/perplexity-prompts.md). Prompt 1 covers FCP plugins and Prompt 2 covers SFX sources. No results are back yet.

## The edit machine (checked 20 Oct 2026)
- Final Cut Pro **12.4** on an Apple **M5 Pro** running macOS 27.0.1. DaVinci Resolve is also installed.
- ⚠️ [[video-edit-pipeline]] says "Resolve or Premiere", but FCP is the working NLE on this Mac, with a full motionVFX kit installed. That page needs updating once the research lands.

## Already installed (`~/Movies/Motion Templates.localized/`)
- **motionVFX:** mCaptions, mTracker 3D (plus Area and Expansions), mTracker Surface, mRotoAI (plus Expansions), mFilmLook, mFlare 2, mPuppet, DesignStudio and Freebies.
- **Pixel Film Studios:** FCPX Stabilizer, Shape Tracker, Timelines and Free Effects/Transitions.
- **Ripple Training (RN):** Shake Transitions, Mask Glow, Effects Expansion Pack, and the Sound Effects packs (8mm, Paper Tear, Glitch, Transitions).
- **Others:** Smooth Camera Zooms Pro, Smooth Transition Layers Free, Target Zoom Transitions, Halation (Lifted Eriks), Cinematic Rotate, BW / Motion Blur 2, Adjustment Layer, and Envato templates (Bento slides, infographic elements, timelines, slideshows, logo openers, number titles).
- Install paths and the Motion nesting rule are in auto-memory `reference-fcp-template-install.md`.

## Already solved locally
- **SFX:** [[sound-lab]] covers custom synth SFX, layering, LUFS levelling, picture-synced cueing and **FCPXML export** of the cues. [[sfx-library]] (Ocular Sounds) covers recorded hits and whooshes.
