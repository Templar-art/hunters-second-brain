# 21 Albany Ave, Currumbin: Main edit SFX + REAL pin

This is [[real-specialists]] (Connor Hewitt)'s listing film. It is the same property as the [[albany-21-day-to-night]] dusk clip, whose twilight front appears at 14.5 s. Job: [JOB.md](../../projects/albany-21-main-sfx/JOB.md). Cost: $0, everything ran locally.

## SFX
This pass reuses the approved [[salerno-st-listing]] v3 recipe: one bed per scene, calibrated to a target distance under the music. The beds are waves on the beaches, wind and birds on the aerials, birds on the exteriors, pool water on the pool shots, and a faint birds bleed on the interiors. There are only four whooshes, one each where the film changes zone, where the pin lands, and on the whip pan. They reuse Hunter's approved Coastal 34 Albany picks ([[auto-sound-design]]). A soft flare plays on the sunburst window.

The music ends at 59.3 s, so the tail gets a fixed-level wind bed fading out under the end card. Whisper and the cicada-band check were both clean.

## REAL pin on the wide drone
- **Finding the house:** in a low sunset oblique, two neighbours look alike, so eyeballing wasn't enough. The cadastre lot plus the QLD aerial photo (roof shape, pool side, the reservoir behind) settled which house it is ([[drone-photo-pin]]).
- **Look:** REAL's own style, the orange logo on the sky with a thin white line down to the roof, centred.
- **Track:** an LK track on the roof ridge.
- **Output:** a 4K ProRes 4444 alpha overlay placed at 00:00:36:08.

## Reel (ambience only)
Hunter added his own whooshes and shings to the reel and asked for ambience only, with **no birds inside the house**. The 17 outdoor shots get calibrated beds. The interiors are left silent, and the outdoor beds end at the cut into them (a 0.12 s fade) instead of crossfading across it.

## Lessons
- **Standing rule (Hunter, 2026-11-09): no birds or ambience on interior shots.** The birds bleed that Salerno used on interiors is out. Interiors stay silent, and an outdoor bed must not crossfade into an interior cut.
- A low sunset oblique makes neighbouring roofs read alike. Confirm the subject from the cadastre and aerial photo by its roof shape and pool side before pinning.
- Check the shot's exact first and last frames from the frame-difference curve. The first track was one frame short at each end.
