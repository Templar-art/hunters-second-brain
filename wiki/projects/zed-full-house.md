## Shape of the job

Consistency is held by anchor-frame transfer — `@original` is the RAW, `@reference` is the approved hero for that space. Anchors: master (#4), living (#6), dining (#9), landing (#13), aerial twilight (#21). Interiors and tight exteriors run full-frame; the twilight aerials and the front elevation run as transfers off the approved aerial rather than fresh generations.

Ten frames are keepers, several redos are out with the client, and the second patio angles were dropped — hero angle only.

## What it taught us

- **Outdoor rooms need outdoor furniture.** Seedream will happily put velvet and marble on a patio. Weatherproof pieces only, and fences and gates must be pinned with "nothing new built" or it invents doors. This became a standing rule ([[staging-rules]]).
- **Run a declutter pass before staging on hard outdoor frames**, rather than asking one prompt to do both.
- **"Makes the room look small" is a geometry note, not a styling note.** The Bedroom 3 revision was answered with a queen bed, a low slim headboard, raised legs with floor visible beneath, a light coverlet and more clear foreground carpet — not with different décor.
- Mirrors are a watch item on every frame that has one; Photoshop is the fallback for a mirror-only fix.
