**Client:** Strategic Investing QLD (@strategicinvesting.qld)

**Site:** strategicinvestingqld.com · **Contact:** 0409 224 488 · info@strategicinvestingqld.com
**Category:** Real Estate Investment Firm (education + strategy, QLD focus)

## Positioning
QLD property investing made clearer — education, strategy and support for new and
experienced investors. Free "strategy chat over coffee". Three-stage program on the site:
(1) understand the financial system, (2) pay off your home faster, (3) build a
multi-property portfolio. Angle: tax-effective strategies + accelerated mortgage freedom.

## Brand system — "Quiet Equity"
Established house style (see [design philosophy](../../projects/strategic-investing-qld/design-philosophy.md)):
- **Navy** `#132A43` (deep ink, holds type) · **Gold** `#B7893B` (burnished, spent rarely)
· **warm cream** (the air) · quiet slate lines.
- **Marcellus** serif headlines — large, mixed-case, never all-caps, gold keyword
highlights. **Inter** for kickers (caps, tracked) + body.
- Drawn flat architecture line-work (pitched-roof homes), banded horizontal composition,
generous margins. Restraint = the visual proof of trust.

## ⚠️ Tone tension
Brief tone brief = **calm, credible, no hype, not guru**. The **website runs bolder**
("80% pay off mortgage in <10yrs", "13,000+ clients"). Social work kept claim-light per
brief; the proof numbers are available if a campaign wants them — but flag compliance
(financial-advice disclaimer) before publishing any hard claim.
