## Brand essentials
- **Colours:** Bespoke Green `#132a24` (primary) · Light Green `#152f2a` (secondary dark — never both in one component) · off-white `#F0EDE8` · greys `#E2DDD7 / #C8C2BB / #7A7470` · body text charcoal `#2A2A2A`. NO accent colours (no gold/terracotta/navy).
- **Type:** **Zodiak Light (300)** all headings (Regular 400 only for legibility; NEVER bold Zodiak) + **Poppins Regular** body / Medium UI / Semibold CTAs. Fallback Georgia.
- **Logo rules:** white on green only (never white on light), clear space = cap-height of B, icon alone only where brand established.
- **Layout:** 8px base unit, generous whitespace mandatory, radius 4px buttons / 8px cards.

## Voice
Trusted local expert; confidence through knowledge, not volume. First person (Jason) for organic. Plain language, one idea per paragraph. **No em dashes** (same as 10x rule). No superlatives ("amazing/incredible/best"), no boastful/corporate/clickbait. Never promise prices/timelines/offer counts (legal risk).

## Caption structure (every caption, 3 parts)
1. **Opening line** — stops the scroll, leads with the point
2. **Value** — 2-4 lines of insight, one idea
3. **Soft invitation** — gentle CTA ("If you're thinking of selling in [area], happy to talk through your options anytime.")

## Social system (strategy PDF, Jun–Dec 2026)
- **2 posts/week, 8/month**: Tue = Reel (2× Authority, 1× Property, 1× Connection) · Thu = static (Property photo, Carousel guide, Jason brand photo, Result/proof). IG primary, FB mirrored.
- **Listing content is a separate layer** per property: New Listing → Open Home → Under Contract → Just Sold.
- **5 pillars:** Market Intelligence 30% · Property Showcase 25% · Behind the Sale 20% · Social Proof 15% · GC Lifestyle 10%.
- **Hooks:** 2-second rule; six templates in guide (pattern interrupt, direct callout, myth buster, data shock, demonstration, social proof open).
- **Video grade:** warm shadows, lifted blacks, slight green tint in shadows; premium-architectural aesthetic, never overproduced. Audio quality above all — reshoot if poor.
- **Ads:** Meta only; audience GC homeowners $1M+ 35-65; CTA "Book a Free Appraisal" / "Get a Market Report"; ads must feel organic.
