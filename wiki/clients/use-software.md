# USE Software

Technology consultancy on the Gold Coast and in Brisbane ([usesoftware.com.au](https://www.usesoftware.com.au)). Speaker on camera: **Sam Bruhn, Founder & CEO**. They run a short-form vertical series called **Definitions**: Sam explains one tech term in about 20 seconds, cut with heavy motion graphics.

**⚠️ Don't confuse with Empire Technologies.** The first reference Hunter sent (an "AI Defence Protocol" webinar promo) was an Empire Technologies ad. v1 copied its branding by mistake. USE's own look comes from their existing Definitions episodes (MCP, API).

## Brand (scraped from their site, 9 Oct 2026)
- Logo: "USE" with a stylised E, blue→cyan gradient. Short version (`USE` only) and long version (`USE software`, grey wordmark). Copies in `projects/use-definitions/brand/`.
- Colours: blue `#009BE8` → cyan `#01E5EE`. Navy `#001C2E`, light grey `#F3F3F3` and ink `#2E3134` come from the episodes.
- Type: **Barlow Condensed** 700/800 for headings, Barlow for body. The episodes add a monospace for labels (JetBrains Mono is the close match).
- Tagline on the end card: "understand · solve · execute".
