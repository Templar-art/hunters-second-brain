## Live ad landing pages (verified 27 Jul 2026)

Both 14 Jul builds **did go live** as dedicated Google Ads landing pages, separate from the site's service pages:
- [/air-conditioning-electrician-gold-coast/](https://mvxelectrical.com.au/air-conditioning-electrician-gold-coast/) — Formidable `form_id 2` (`air-con`)
- [/gold-coast-electricians/](https://mvxelectrical.com.au/gold-coast-electricians/) — Formidable `form_id 3` (`general-electrician`)

Design shipped to spec (message-matched H1, 7 tap-to-call, sticky call bar, licence strip, "one trade, one visit"). **⚠️ The form did not** — the Elementor/Formidable build re-added email + postcode as required and made the free-text message required, giving six required fields where the approved spec was four with email deleted. Site nav also still present on both (spec said strip it). Fix list: [FORM-FIX.md](../../projects/mvx-lead-fix/FORM-FIX.md). Lesson: a shipped page is not a shipped spec — re-read the live markup after someone else rebuilds it.

**⚠️ No hot water surface exists.** Checked the full 29-page sitemap: zero hot water / heat pump pages, nav items or mentions, including on both ad LPs. Matt wants to shift lead mix from aircon toward hot water electrical systems — there is currently nothing for ads to point at or Google to rank, so the absence of hot water leads is structural, not a performance problem. **Blocked:** MVX holds electrical/ARC/solar/cabler licences but no plumbing licence, and the water side of a HWS swap is licensed plumbing in QLD — need Matt's answer on subbing vs partnering a plumber before building the page or the campaign. Angle if it proceeds: heat pump attracts the federal STC rebate (~$998, applied as an upfront discount), and heat-pump/electric-storage work is genuinely electrician-led.

## Work

- **2026-08-10 — General Electrical campaign built as a *calls* campaign, not forms.** [BUILD-CALLS.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/BUILD-CALLS.md). The 3 Aug shoot delivered **three** talking-head cuts (10.1s / 12.3s / 15.2s, all 1080×1920, same shirt and location, interchangeable) plus two commercial project videos — Green Power Boxes and a Broadbeach Library fitout — which are organic content, not ad creative: a homeowner with a dead downlight doesn't care about a council library. **All three ads are one angle**, the General Electrical "list" frame, varying only the hook, exactly as [VIDEO-SCRIPTS.md](../../projects/mvx-creatives/VIDEO-SCRIPTS.md) built them. ⚠️ **Setup B never happened — there is no new-build site footage**, so [BUILD-NEWBUILDS.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/BUILD-NEWBUILDS.md) stays unbuilt and must not be launched off the reno cut. **The decisive finding: every cut ends on "give us a call", not "tap below".** Conversion location locks at publish, so the mechanism had to match the creative — Hunter's call, 10 Aug: **Calls**, $50/day, Advantage+ audience **ON** (which also settles the last open item in [ACCOUNT-SETTINGS.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/ACCOUNT-SETTINGS.md), now locked account-wide). Choosing calls **deletes the instant form and every qualifier in it**, so quality control moves to the performance goal: optimise for **quality calls (≥60s duration)**, never call count — a duration threshold measures commitment where the budget-band question only asked about it. Structure supersedes BUILD.md's five-ad-set floors *for this campaign only* — three hooks on one angle are compared by link CTR and thumbstop, both impression-normalised, so they don't need equal spend; one ad set concentrates the learning instead of fragmenting it. ⚠️ **Two new risks the form spec didn't have:** Meta only allows dayparting on a lifetime budget, so a call ad runs 24/7 (launching that way deliberately, hour-of-day breakdown at day 7 decides whether to switch), and **a missed call is a wasted click with no record** — needs Matt's answer on who answers the phone and what the voicemail says before this is anything other than an accepted risk.
- **2026-07-29 — Video ad scripts + shoot brief (filming Mon 3 Aug).** Two Meta video ads, 20s each, Matt to camera: **general electrical** and **new builds & renos**. One brief covers the lot — [MVX-Video-Shoot-Brief.pdf](../../projects/mvx-creatives/MVX-Video-Shoot-Brief.pdf), built from [brief.html](../../projects/mvx-creatives/brief.html) via Chrome headless, sendable to Matt and the crew as-is. Built as 3 opening lines + 1 middle + 2 sign-offs per ad = 12 takes, so the body and CTA are constant and only the hook varies; that matters because the day-7 kill rule in [BUILD.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/BUILD.md) reads link CTR, which is almost entirely the first three seconds. Vary hook and body together and a losing ad can't tell you which half lost. **General electrical was reframed off symptoms onto a planned-work "the list" angle** — the paused campaign sold emergencies, emergencies go to Google, and Meta only caught the $200-clean end at 3 leads/$89. Copy written to be *said*, not read: sentence fragments, "sparky", "flick us a message", noun lists cut to three. Two lines need Matt's sign-off before filming ("we turn up when we say we will"; lighting/power *layout* not *design*). ⚠️ **Shoot dependency: site access for the new-build setup** — fallback is plans on a ute tailgate. ⚠️ **Unresolved:** BUILD.md and [BUILD-NEWBUILDS.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/BUILD-NEWBUILDS.md) disagree on structure, budget and Advantage+ audience (OFF vs ON) — scripts don't depend on it, ad set builds do.
- **2026-07-29 — Pre-launch settings from an outside operator.** [[zidane-meta-ads-tips]]. Three changes to make before the rebuilt campaign publishes. **Advantage+ creative enhancements (text improvements / visual improvements) must be OFF** — they let Meta rewrite ad copy per placement, which would silently undo the scope-based qualification the whole rebuild depends on, and the resulting junk leads would read as a targeting failure. **The dataset (formerly Pixel) wants installing and verified now**, ahead of the campaign it is meant to inform, since a fresh one has no event history during exactly the week the campaign gets judged. CBO at campaign level is confirmed — already the spec. **Dynamic UTMs do not apply here**: the rebuild uses on-Facebook instant forms, so no URL loads and there are no parameters to fill; that string belongs on the Google Ads landing pages instead.
- **2026-07-28 — Meta rebuild build sheet.** [BUILD.md](../../projects/mvx-meta-rebuild/BUILD.md). Both existing campaigns produced low-quality leads: 13 leads / ~$541 over 30 days, ~a third outside the service area (Sydney, Canberra, Brisbane postcodes), most of the rest small or supply-your-own-unit jobs. Root cause was the ad set, not the creative: performance goal "maximise number of leads" hunts the cheapest form-fillers, audience was 3.8-4.5M against a ~700k service area, Advantage+ audience on, and the instant form prefilled everything with no qualifying questions. Meta locks conversion location + performance goal after publish, so both campaigns get deleted and rebuilt. New spec: one campaign, geo locked to "people living in this location", Advantage+ off, cost-per-result goal set so Meta stops chasing the floor, and a form that asks supply-vs-BYO-unit plus a budget band with phone prefill OFF. Four ad angles, kill rules at day 7, scale the survivor. **No pricing in ads** (Hunter, 28 Jul) - filtering happens via scope publicly and budget bands privately. Switch to conversion-leads optimisation once Lach's call tracking feeds lead status back.
