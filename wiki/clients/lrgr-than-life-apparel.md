# LRGR Than Life Apparel (Larger Than Life Apparel)

Streetwear brand whose hero product is deep-fitting A-frame hats. The founder, Shak, is also the face of the brand. Positioning is quality and fit first, with no hype. Voice: casual, direct, Australian. Storefront: https://lrgrthanlifeapparel.com/

## Creative direction
Source: the creative brief in [raw/lrgr/](../../raw/lrgr/LRGR_Claude_Creative_Brief.pdf), dated 2026-10-18. The core principle is **proof over promises**. Shak is cast as the person who shows you what to look for in a hat, not a founder calling his product premium. Content pillars: The Hat Test, Streetwear Built For Us, and Side by Side Comparison.

Key insight: a hat that fits *around* the head isn't necessarily one that sits *down* on it. That difference shows in side profile, not from the front. Don't claim deeper is always better.

## v2 plan (2026-10-18)
[LRGR-CREATIVE-PLAN-v2.md](../../projects/streetwear-ads/LRGR-CREATIVE-PLAN-v2.md) is a critical rework of the client PDF. It found that 9 of the 12 original concepts leaned on side profile or crown depth, and that the scripts pre-wrote participant verdicts. The new six are Red or Blue? (a blind pick), Ours Or Not? (a feel-box game), The Tug Count, Mark It Up (tape self-audit), The Drawer Hat and In The Bag (packed at arrival, unpacked at wrap). **Hard constraint from Hunter: everything must be filmable in one session**, so there are no series or multi-day tests. What Goes First? (30 wears) was cut for that reason. Client PDF: `LRGR Video Concepts v2.pdf`, with no pricing and no internal critique. It also has lean and expanded shoot schedules and a client checklist. Lesson: a client deliverable must never script participant reactions or assume the client's product wins a test.
