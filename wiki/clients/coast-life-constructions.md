## The diagnosis (one line)
Real builder, real projects, real trade network — but the account reads as a **tradie mate's page**, not a builder homeowners hire for a $1M+ custom build. Commenters are roofers/pumpies, not homeowners or designers.

## The three positioning shifts
1. **Name every project** — every build gets a name, used in every post/hashtag/story. Retroactively name the last 3 finished builds. (Barlow's biggest lever: ZARMA, Kokomo, Bon Sol, Aruma.)
2. **Rebalance pillars** — from 100% BTS to **40% finished builds / 25% BTS / 20% design-process / 15% people**.
3. **Caption voice upgrade** — ban "long days / on the tools / boys getting into it". Design-story, material-specific captions (Barlow style) but keep the plainspoken Aussie identity.

## Explicitly NOT recommended
- Don't kill BTS entirely (reduce to 25%, it's a real asset)
- Don't chase Grimmond overnight (12-month target is Barlow)
- Don't rebrand the handle
- Don't spend on ads until pillars are rebalanced (ads would amplify the wrong audience)

## Brand voice file — key contents (03)
- **Positioning:** move toward Barlow's territory **without losing the plainspoken Gold Coast identity** — every caption should sit on Barlow's grid yet keep Coast Life hands-on-honesty.
- **Audience:** GC/SEQ homeowners, $600k+ budget, age 35–60; secondary: investors + local architects/designers.
- **Voice rules:** confident not cocky; craftsmanship-forward ("Blackbutt hardwood, hand-oiled" beats "premium timber finish"); design-intent language (why, not just what); family-run warmth when natural; no hype.
- **Banned:** "long days and steady work", "on the tools again today", "gear on site", "boys getting straight into it", plus brochure-speak ("dream home", "elevate", "DM to inquire"…).
- **Signature phrases:** "Built the right way." / "The way we'd build our own." / "Coast Life — from foundations to final finish."
- **Visual grade:** warm neutrals (honey oak, travertine, cream, charcoal) — Barlow's grade. No teal-and-orange, no moody underexposure. Grid consistency beats volume. Feeds into Higgsfield work.
- **Hashtags:** rotate menus (location ×2, category ×2, material ×1, brand always); **retire #OnSite #OnTheTools immediately**.

## Barlow's actual layout system — measured, not guessed (1 Sep 2026)
Hunter supplied a @barlowbuilding carousel as the reference. Measured off the source files by pixel-scanning, not eyeballed. Full write-up: `projects/clc-graphic-sprint-sep-2026/REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md`.
- **They post 3:4 (1080×1440), not 4:5.** ⚠️ Supersedes the 1080×1350 assumption in [[clc-brand-voice]] §11 and every earlier CLC graphic. They're taking the extra feed height.
- **The ground colour is sampled from each post's own photograph** and held muted (desaturated, mid value). Measured: `#ada79b` greige · `#3d4d42` deep green · `#788097` blue-grey · `#6c7079` slate · `#8e7b5a` tan · `#222931` near-black. **This is the single mechanism that makes their grid read as one book** — not a fixed palette, a production rule. It was never identified in the July field research, which called it "one consistent grade".
- **Hierarchy is inverted:** brand mark on top (large, tone-on-tone), image inset second, tiny tracked all-caps caption third. No headline, no counters, no eyebrows, no CTA slide. Every slide in a carousel is identical bar photo + caption.
- **Rhythm:** image 636×696 inset with 20.6% side margins, and four near-equal ~150px vertical rests. The surround is what makes it read as a plate.
- Copy voice on this post: long sensory caption ending in a brand line ("Built with intention. Always.").
CLC canvas rebuilt on these mechanics in CLC's own brand (white master, Poppins, grounds sampled from CLC photography). Old 4:5 generator preserved at `canvas/build-4x5.py.bak`.
