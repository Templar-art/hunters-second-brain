# Source — Strategic Investing QLD, Social Post Brief (Batch 1)

**Original:** `raw/` (dropped 15 Jul 2026) — "Strategic Investing QLD — Claude Design
Social Post Brief". Client: [[strategic-investing-qld]].

## What it asked for
First batch of **9 IG/FB posts** to make the profile look credible and strategy-led from
day one. Audience: everyday Aussies into QLD property investing (beginners → experienced).
Tone: calm, credible, professional, strategic — **not hypey, not guru**.

Requested visual direction: **navy + gold**, clean modern editorial / financial-services
feel, strong sans-serif hierarchy, generous margins, subtle line work, cohesive 3×3 grid,
mobile readability first. Avoid teal/neon/purple gradients, wealth clichés (cars,
mansions, dollar signs), stock-photo cheese.

The 9 posts (title · format): 1 Welcome (static) · 2 Who this is for (carousel) · 3 Why
QLD (static/carousel) · 4 Common mistakes (carousel) · 5 Case study (carousel) · 6 What
happens in a strategy chat (static) · 7 The right team (carousel/static) · 8 Strategy Tip
#1 (static) · 9 Book a chat / CTA (static). Each with headline, visual direction, caption.

## How it was actioned
Content adopted as written. **Departed from the brief's "sans-serif / navy-base" note** in
favour of the client's **already-established Quiet Equity house system** (Marcellus serif,
cream + navy #132A43 + gold #B7893B) — Hunter's call, for brand consistency with the
earlier ad kit. Built a re-runnable PIL generator; delivered 20 slides, 0 credits.
See [[strategic-investing-qld]] · JOB.md · captions.md in `projects/strategic-investing-qld/`.

## Flags carried forward
- **⚠️** Brief's calm/no-hype tone vs the website's bolder claims — kept posts claim-light.
- Typographic brand mark is a placeholder until the real SIQ logo lands.
