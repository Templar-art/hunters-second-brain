## A. Barlow (@barlowbuilding, 17.3k)

### 1. The origin-story site post — instagram.com/barlowbuilding/p/DP17ujBk-oV/
Pinned. Drone of DAAS Estate site, slab #1 complete. 19 comments, 2 shares.
- Caption: "There are some sites that just feel different… For Sam and me, this land has always been special. We both grew up around here, going past this block as kids on the water…" ends "Who can guess where it is? 👀"
- **Works:** personal founder story attached to a muddy-site photo makes PROGRESS content feel premium; ends with an engagement question; tags **21 trade partners** (fuller_electrical, modinexgroup, a_plus_plastering, voswoodfloors, glux, cdkstone, polytec, render.x, melvilleandodd, custom_stone_solutions, kinnectbricklaying, upinstone_goldcoast, ruya_aluminium, surfcitywelding21 + more); location-tagged Broadbeach Waters; comments are trades AND real-estate agents.
- **CLC version:** Ladds Ridge slab/pad post with Rob's story about the site + "who can guess the suburb?" + every trade tagged. This is BTS done the Barlow way — same photo CLC already posts, completely different result.

### 2. The sensory manifesto (pinned hero) — instagram.com/barlowbuilding/p/DQm1RXMkxu7/
45 comments, 4 shares — their engagement ceiling.
- "There's a feeling you get when you walk into a BARLOW home… the weight of the materials, the way light moves through the space… ARUMA Avenue — a home that reminds us why we build differently."
- **Works:** zero specs, pure feeling; project name as sign-off; a designer (@la_loma_house_) asks WHERE THE STONE IS FROM in comments = supplier-grade engagement.
- **CLC version:** the Chambers Lane reveal caption already drafted follows this shape. Once material names are confirmed, expect supplier questions in comments — answer them publicly.

### 3. The 3-word editorial — instagram.com/barlowbuilding/p/DZy1KrmFCzK/
- Caption entire: "Translating intent into reality." Design-process photo (team at drawings wall). **Collab post co-authored with @modinexgroup** so it sits on the supplier's grid too.
- **Works:** brevity = confidence; the collab doubles reach for free.
- **Doesn't work for CLC yet:** you need brand recognition before 3-word captions carry; CLC should use short+one-substance-line for now.
- **CLC version:** Rob + client over plans at the Ladds Ridge site: "Getting it right on paper first." — collab-invite Bidcrete or the engineer.

### 4. The sub-account project post — instagram.com/namiya_bybarlow/p/DOfdB7gkdnT/
- NAMIYA (project has its own account). "…raw stone, warm timber, and glass that captures light… Designed by @reecekeildesign for our wonderful clients."
- **Works:** material trio named; designer credited (architects reshare when credited); "for our wonderful clients" = warmth without naming names.
- **Doesn't (for CLC):** project sub-accounts are overkill below ~10k. Steal the caption grammar, skip the infrastructure.

### 5. The trade interview series — instagram.com/by_barlow/reel/DaYxiDNzvvq/
- "In Conversation With BARLOW": Sam Fuller of Fuller Electrical on what a long-term builder-trade relationship looks like. Collab post.
- **Works:** positions the builder as the hub of a quality network; the trade reshares it; infinitely repeatable format.
- **CLC version (month 3+):** "On site with…" — Rob + one trade, 60s, one question: "why do we keep working together?" 10x films it in the monthly batch day.

## The 6 steals, ranked by effort-to-impact
1. Tag every trade, every post (Barlow does 21) + location tag
2. Project name in every caption, reveal formula "Welcome to / At the heart of [NAME]"
3. Founder story on BTS posts (turns existing content premium)
4. Collab-post suppliers and 10x (both grids, free reach)
5. Teaser → reveal arcs
6. Credit block incl. @10xvisuals.co (Grimmond credits their media team — CLC should credit ours)
