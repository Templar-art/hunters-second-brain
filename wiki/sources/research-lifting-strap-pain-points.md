# Research: lifting strap pain points (2026-09-30)

Desk research by a Claude subagent for [[one-percent-standard-store]]. Sources were Amazon AU product pages (including Amazon's per-topic sentiment counts), review sites, brand sites and one batch of r/StrongerByScience comments. Reddit itself was mostly blocked, so the counts are indicative, not statistical.

## Complaints, most frequent first
1. **Durability failure.** This is the top complaint for cuffed D-ring straps: D-rings tearing off, stitching failing, and hook-and-loop letting go mid-set. Gymreapers GripLink, the closest match to One Percent, has 29% one-star reviews on Amazon AU, and 31 of its 36 durability mentions are negative. [Amazon AU](https://www.amazon.com.au/dp/B0G44W1DD3)
2. **Fit and sizing.** Mostly a loop and figure-8 problem. The adjustable cuff sidesteps it.
3. **Slipping.** Often the lifter is wrapping the strap the wrong way.
4. **Wrist pain.** The D-ring or a seam digs in.
5. **Length.** Even a 51cm strap draws "too short" complaints. One Percent's is 30cm, so test it on thick bars before claiming it fits everything.
6. **Not knowing how to use straps.**
7. **Sweat and smell.** Neoprene holds odour.
8. **"Straps are cheating" and grip-strength worries.** This is a content theme more than a complaint.

## Gaps nobody owns
- Proven durability for cuffed D-ring straps.
- An Australian brand with local stock and local replacements.
- A how-to that ships in the box.
- A calm training-habit voice rather than the hardcore "reaper" tone.

## Recommended angle
**"The cuffed strap that doesn't quit,"** with a "your first straps, done right" education line as the content engine. It hits the category's biggest documented failure and the market leader's weakest point.

⚠️ **Precondition:** inspect how One Percent's own D-ring is attached, and abuse-test it on camera, before making any durability claim. If the construction matches GripLink's, the angle backfires. Budget for a local replacement promise, for example 12 months with the replacement shipped from the Gold Coast. Other angles on the table:
- one strap for the whole session
- a fit range published honestly
- local delivery, as a supporting point only
