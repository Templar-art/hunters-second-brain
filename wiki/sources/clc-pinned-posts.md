# Coast Life Constructions — 3 Pinned Post Concepts

_Part of the [[coast-life-constructions]] CLC pack — start at [[clc-pack-readme]]; frozen originals in `raw/clc/`._
_Managed by 10x Visuals | These anchor the profile top row. Refresh every 90 days._
_Benchmark: @barlowbuilding's grid opens with editorial finished-build hero shots plus text-overlay design statements. Coast Life's current top row is BTS drone + on-site workers, which reads as "we do construction" not "we build custom homes." These 3 pins fix that in the first 30 days._

The pinned row is a first-time visitor's 3-second decision. Each pin does one job:
1. **Show what they build** (aspiration)
2. **Show who they are** (trust)
3. **Show how they think** (authority)

---

## PIN 1 — "The Signature Build" (Aspiration)

**Format:** Cinematic Reel, 30–45s, 9:16 with 4:3 cover
**Pillar:** Project showcase
**Purpose:** In 5 seconds, a stranger should know these are premium custom builders.

**Concept:**
Locked-off hero shots of your best completed build. Time-flow AI shot (dawn → dusk on the exterior). Close-ups on 3 signature material moments. End on the owner walking through with the client at handover.

**Cover text overlay:** Project name + suburb (e.g. "The Mermaid Beach Build")
**Caption hook:** "18 months. 40 tradies. One family home built the way we'd build our own."
**CTA:** "Full walkthrough on our site."

---

## PIN 2 — "Meet Coast Life" (Trust)

**Format:** Reel, 45–60s, talking-head + B-roll
**Pillar:** People / brand story
**Purpose:** Put the family/owners on camera so this stops feeling like a corporate account.

**Concept:**
Owner talking to camera on an active site. Answers three questions in cuts:
1. "Why we started Coast Life"
2. "What we won't compromise on"
3. "The kind of client we love working with"

B-roll: team on the tools, dog on site, morning site walk-through.

**Cover text overlay:** "Meet the builders"
**Caption hook:** "We started Coast Life because we were tired of watching builders cut corners on other people's homes."
**CTA:** "New consults booking now."

---

## PIN 3 — "How We Actually Build" (Authority)

**Format:** Carousel, 6–8 slides, 4:3
**Pillar:** Educational
**Purpose:** Signal expertise. Give a first-time visitor a reason to save and share.

**Concept:**
Slide-by-slide breakdown of your build process — from initial site meeting through handover. Each slide: one big photo from a real build + one short line of text.

**Slide structure:**
1. Hook: "How a Coast Life home actually gets built."
2. Stage 1 — Design & site assessment
3. Stage 2 — Materials sourcing (name your suppliers)
4. Stage 3 — Framing & structure
5. Stage 4 — Fit-out & finish
6. Stage 5 — Handover
7. The 3 things we do differently
8. CTA: "See the current builds on our stories."

**Caption hook:** "Every builder says 'we're different.' Here's what we actually mean by it."
**CTA:** "Save this if you're planning a build."

---

## Production priority
Film PIN 2 first (fastest to shoot, highest trust-lift). PIN 1 requires a completed build handover moment — line it up around your next handover date. PIN 3 can be built from existing photo library + fresh captions this week.
