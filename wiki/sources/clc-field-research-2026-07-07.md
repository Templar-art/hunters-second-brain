# CLC Field Research — Live Instagram Study (7 Jul 2026)

_Part of the [[coast-life-constructions]] CLC pack — start at [[clc-pack-readme]]; frozen originals in `raw/clc/`._

First-hand study of benchmark + adjacent accounts via live feeds (not just the Perplexity audit). Feeds into [[clc-strategy-guide]] execution, and its "6 steals" are cross-referenced in [[clc-swipe-file-2026-07-07]]. Studied: @barlowbuilding, @grimmondau, @bungalow_home, @smout_property. (@matt_srama checked — buyers agent, not a builder reference.)

## @barlowbuilding — 17.3K followers, 384 posts (the target)

**Bio architecture:** brand mark "BARLOW" + routing lines: "Behind the scenes @by_barlow / Projects rising now @somewhere_bybarlow". The main grid stays pure editorial because BTS overflow lives on sub-accounts. CLC lesson: grid purity — not sub-accounts (too early) but strict curation.

**Story highlights = project archive.** One highlight per named project (NAMIYA, Alchemy, AXL, Mexicali, Kokomo, Aruma, Zarma), every cover a "B." monogram on a material swatch. The profile reads as a portfolio the second it loads.

**3 pinned posts = range statement:** (1) drone of a site starting, (2) finished white coastal exterior, (3) finished dark-timber + pool exterior. Progress + two contrasting finished heroes.

**"In Conversation With BARLOW"** — reel series interviewing their own trade partners (Fuller Electrical, Reece Keil Design). Turns suppliers into content, tags, and reshare machines simultaneously.

**Collab posts with suppliers** — e.g. design-process post co-authored with @modinexgroup via IG Collab, so it sits on both grids. Beyond tagging: shared grid real estate.

**Caption modes (verified verbatim):**
- Ultra-short editorial: *"Translating intent into reality."* (design-process post)
- Sensory long-form ending in the project name: *"There's a feeling you get when you walk into a BARLOW home… It's in the weight of the materials, the way light moves through the space… ARUMA Avenue — a home that reminds us why we build differently."*
- Manifesto text-overlays: "BUILDING IN SERVICE OF THE DESIGN", "PLANNED WITH INTENT. BUILT THE SAME WAY."

**Fun fact:** already followed by @10xvisuals.co, @tysonscheuner and @cva__architects.

## @grimmondau — 8.3K followers, 478 posts (the ceiling)

- Bio: tagline **"FOREVER. IN. FORM."** + **"Owned by @nathan.grimmond"** + street address. Founder-forward + physical-address legitimacy.
- **Founder on camera** — walkthrough reels with Nathan talking to camera. The personal brand carries the account. CLC equivalent: Rob.
- Teaser captions: *"Dream penthouse. Full reveal coming soon."* — teaser → reveal sequencing as a deliberate arc.
- Highlights: lowercase project names with a full stop (sempre. luxe. lusso. amalfi. tres. monaco. ora.) on colour-swatch covers.

Not a builder — use as the visual-book reference. Named-project highlights again (PRESS, ROCA, ALBI, BIGHT, VERDE, SAVOY). Sub-brand ("BH STUDIOS"). Confirms: the named-project highlight system is the industry-standard premium pattern, not a Barlow quirk.

## @smout_property — 22.4K (GC/Brisbane developer couple)
- **Faces as the brand** — the couple IS the profile photo; personality-led everything.
- Press proof line in bio: "As Seen In: Grand Designs, Home Beautiful, Adore."
- Named projects: Corazòn, The Gables (highlights).
- POV-format reels: *"POV: you just found Brisbane's newest luxury shoot location."*

## The cross-account formula (what actually works — every account does these)
1. **Named projects with highlight archives** — all 4 accounts. The single most consistent pattern.
2. **A human face on the account** — Nathan (Grimmond), the Smouts, Barlow's conversation series. Faceless builder accounts don't hit these numbers.
3. **Captions are editorial, never activity logs** — either ultra-short declarative or sensory story ending in the project name.
4. **Trade partners as content, not just tags** — Barlow's interview series + IG Collab co-authored posts.
5. **Pins curate the range** — progress + finished contrast.
6. **One consistent grade** — the grid reads as one book.
7. **Teaser → reveal arcs** — Grimmond explicitly, Barlow implicitly (site drone pin → project highlight).

## CLC rip-off actions (in order of effort-to-impact)
1. **Rebuild story highlights as named projects** — Chambers Lane, Burleigh, Gundara, Winship, Ladds Ridge — with "CL." monogram covers on material swatches (matches the CLC social graphics template). Zero new content needed; pure repackaging.
2. **Pinned posts per Barlow's structure:** 1 drone site-progress + 2 contrasting finished heroes (fits the pack's pinned_posts.md concepts).
3. **Use IG Collab (co-author), not just tags**, on every post featuring a trade's work — start with the 3 known trades.
4. **"On site with Rob" monthly reel** — Grimmond's founder-on-camera pattern; also seeds the audit's people pillar (15%).
5. **Teaser → reveal arc for each named-project backfill** — 1 detail teaser ("Chambers Lane. This week.") then the hero carousel.
6. **Long-term (12mo+): trade-partner interview series** — CLC's version of "In Conversation" — 10x shoots it; deepens trade relationships and feeds the network effect.
