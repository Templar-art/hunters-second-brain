# Post-Production

Everything that happens after the shoot: making stills and video match, look premium, and ship per-platform.

## Child notes
- [[photo-editing-pipeline]] — real-estate/architectural stills in Lightroom + Photoshop.
- [[color-grading]] — grading video (and stills consistency); the "one grade" rule.
- [[video-edit-pipeline]] — property/social video end-to-end.

## House rules
- Shoot RAW (stills) / log (video) for latitude. Protect highlights on windows and skies.
- One consistent grade across a set — [[brand-system]]. No shot-to-shot drift.
- Non-destructive: virtual copies, adjustment layers/smart objects, grade on nodes.
- Match before you stylise. Balance neutral first, then apply look.
- Export per-platform: correct resolution, aspect, codec, fps, loudness.
