# Client Onboarding

Getting a new client running smoothly — from signed to steady output.

## Intake

Capture up front:

- Goals — what does winning look like (sales, leads, brand awareness)?
- Brand assets — logos, fonts, colour, existing photo/video library.
- Access — accounts (IG, FB, ad manager), scheduling tools, drives.
- Examples they like — reference accounts, saved posts, "make me look like this".
- Non-negotiables — off-limits topics, must-include elements, hard brand rules.

## Discovery / brand-voice capture

Study before you make anything.

- Read their feed voice before writing a single caption — see [[ig-caption-voice]].
- Capture the brand rules (visual system, tone, do/don't) — see [[brand-system]].
- Output a short brand card the whole workflow can reference.

## Kickoff deliverables

- Content plan — themes, formats, cadence.
- Shoot schedule — dates, locations, shot list.
- First batch — get something live fast to build trust.

## Expectations

Agree these in writing before work starts:

- Turnaround times.
- Revision rounds (match what [[pricing-and-packaging]] scoped).
- Approval flow — who reviews, who signs off. Name the decision-maker. (e.g. at [[coast-life-constructions]], Rob's brand verdict governs — one authority, no committee.)

## Asset & file handoff

- Set delivery format and location (drive folder, naming convention).
- Confirm they can receive, store, and repost without friction.

## Review cadence

Set a recurring check-in from day one so feedback is scheduled, not reactive.

## Job tracking

Every job gets a `JOB.md` (the Jarvis job-tracking system) — the single source of truth for status, scope, deliverables, and decisions on that job. Don't let it live in chat threads.

## Into steady state

Once the first batch lands and the approval flow works, transition from onboarding into ongoing [[posting-cadence]] and the wider [[content-systems]] engine.
