# Exterior Architecture

Facades, drone, sky, and materials — showing the building's form.

## Framing the facade

- **Straight-on (one-point)** — square to the facade, sensor parallel. Formal, graphic, catalogue hero.
- **Three-quarter (two-point)** — the depth/volume hero. Shows two faces and how the building sits. Usually the stronger marketing image.

## Separate the building from clutter

- Move or plan to clone out: **cars, bins, wires, hoses, signage**.
- Reframe so rooflines don't merge with **power lines, trees, or neighbours**.
- Watch the **base** of the frame — driveways and gutters full of junk kill a hero shot.

## Sky

- Shoot when the **sky has interest** (broken cloud, colour), or replace/enhance in post.
- Don't over-drown the subject in empty sky.
- Sky replacement / AI enhancement is a valid finishing step — see [[staging-rules]].

## Light

- **Golden hour** rakes facade texture and materials.
- **Blue hour / twilight** is the premium exterior — lights on, deep sky. See [[light-and-timing]].
- Remember southern-hemisphere sun path: north facades sunlit most of day; south facades shaded → overcast or twilight.

## Drone for architecture

- **Low obliques** to show form, massing, and how the building meets the site.
- **Straight-down** for site plans, roof, and pool/landscape layout.
- **Keep verticals straight** — face the facade **square-on** and keep the gimbal level; don't shoot up at an angle that keystones the building.
- **CASA rules (Australia)** — fly under the sub-2kg / commercial rules as they apply: register, hold the appropriate accreditation/licence for paid work, respect **altitude limits (120 m AGL)**, maintain **visual line of sight**, stay clear of controlled airspace and people. Check restrictions per site before flying.

## Shadows & detail

- **Harsh shadows** — shoot the shaded facade at overcast or twilight, or on the sunlit side during raking light. Avoid midday hard-shadow chop.
- **Detail shots** — materials, cladding junctions, entry/threshold, texture. These sell craftsmanship and pad the gallery.

## AI note

- ❌ **Never AI full-frame a drone wide** — hallucinated geometry ruins architectural credibility. See [[higgsfield-pipelines]].
