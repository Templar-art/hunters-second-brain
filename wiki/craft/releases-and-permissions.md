# Releases and Permissions

Permissions, releases, and rights for architectural/property work in Australia.

**Not legal advice — verify current law/CASA rules and get proper advice for commercial jobs.**

## Photographing buildings
- Under the Australian Copyright Act (ss 65–66) you can generally photograph and publish images of **buildings** and **public sculptures/works of artistic craftsmanship on permanent public display** without infringing the architect's/artist's copyright. Australia has effective **"freedom of panorama."**
- So the building itself is usually fine to shoot and publish.
- This is about copyright — it does **not** override access rules or privacy (below).

## Property access / release
- Private property is about **permission to enter and shoot** — a physical-access matter, not copyright.
- Get the owner's or agent's OK before entering. For occupied homes, confirm they consent to photos being published.
- Some venues/strata/managed properties restrict commercial shoots — check.
- Shooting from a public street of a private building is generally allowed; entering the land to shoot needs permission.

## Image ownership / licensing
- The **photographer holds copyright by default** unless assigned in writing.
- Client gets a **licence** to use the images per the agreement — not ownership, unless you sell/assign it.
- Set usage terms (scope, duration, media) in the client agreement. See [[client-onboarding]] and [[pricing-and-packaging]].
- Extra/unagreed use = extra licence fee.

## Model releases
- Needed for **identifiable people** where images are used **commercially / in advertising**.
- Editorial/news use has more latitude, but for ads get a signed model release.
- Incidental crowd/street people generally lower risk, but avoid featuring recognisable individuals in ad material without a release.

## Drone permissions (Australia / CASA)
Commercial drone work needs CASA compliance. General baseline:
- **Register** the drone (commercial).
- Hold **operator accreditation**; **RePL** (and operate under a **ReOC**) where required by weight/operation.
- Fly within **standard operating conditions**: ≤120m AGL, visual line of sight (VLOS), daytime, clear of people/populous areas, clear of controlled/restricted airspace (near aerodromes etc.).
- **Landowner permission** to take off/land.
- Respect **neighbours' privacy** — don't hover over/into adjoining properties.
- **VERIFY CURRENT CASA RULES at casa.gov.au before any paid flight** — categories, weights, and airspace rules change.
- See [[exterior-architecture]] for aerial exterior work.

## Trademarks / branding in frame
- Visible logos/branded goods in **advertising** images can raise trademark/passing-off issues. Fine in most editorial/listing contexts; for ads, avoid or clear prominent third-party branding.
