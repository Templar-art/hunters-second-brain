# Awards & Competitions

## Why enter
- **Credibility** — third-party validation clients and agents trust.
- **Portfolio pressure** — deadlines force personal-best work, not just paid volume.
- **Marketing proof** — "award-winning" and shortlist badges support premium pricing. See [[real-estate-vs-architectural]].
- **Networking & PR** — press, features, peer contacts.
- Feeds the brand narrative and [[10x-ig-repositioning]].

## Competitions to consider
Categories, sponsors, and branding shift year to year — treat this as a starting list.
- **Architectural Photography Awards (APA)** — long-linked to ArchDaily; dedicated architecture categories.
- **Sony World Photography Awards** — architecture category, high profile.
- **International Photography Awards (IPA)** — broad, with architecture/built-environment categories.
- **Australian pro photography awards** — EPSON / AIPP-lineage national and state bodies; relevant as a Gold Coast / AU operator.
- **Architizer-related and building-specific photo prizes** — tied to architecture media/practices.
- **VERIFY current entry rules, categories, fees, and deadlines before entering** — these change annually.

## Submission craft
- Enter your **strongest 1–3 images**, not a dump.
- Read the **category brief** and answer it precisely.
- Where a series is allowed, **sequence** it deliberately.
- Flawless execution — perfect verticals, clean grade, resolved detail. This is where [[tilt-shift-technique]], [[architectural-details]], and [[color-grading]] pay off.
- Respect **image-integrity / manipulation rules** — some awards limit compositing and heavy retouching.
- Secure any permissions needed for buildings/interiors before submitting. See [[releases-and-permissions]].

## Leveraging a win or shortlist
- Case-study post across channels.
- Bio and website line ("award-winning / shortlisted").
- Client pitches and proposals.
- Local PR / press outreach.

## Cadence
Target **1–2 entries a year**, built around personal projects rather than client jobs. Keep a running shortlist of frames worth entering.

> Verify current competitions, categories, and rules before entering.
