# Reel Formats

Repeatable Instagram Reel / short-form formats for a property, construction, and architecture studio. Pick an archetype, shoot to it, produce fast.

## The hook (first 1–3s)
- Non-negotiable. The scroll stops here or not at all.
- Visual hook: the single best frame first. Hero drone push, glass reveal, a striking line of the build.
- Text hook: bold on-screen line that creates a question or promise ("This was an empty slab 6 months ago").
- Pattern interrupt: motion, scale, or contrast in frame 1. No slow logo intros, no dead ramp-up.
- First frame doubles as the cover/thumbnail. Make it earn the tap.

## Format archetypes
- **Before/after reveal.** Render → real. Empty → staged. Slab → finished. The reveal is the payoff — cut on the beat.
- **Walkthrough / transformation.** Continuous move through the space; energy builds to the hero room.
- **Satisfying build progression.** Time-lapse or stitched stages of the build. Momentum + completion = watch-through.
- **Single hero shot + bold caption.** One flawless clip, one strong on-screen line. Restraint reads premium.
- **Carousel of stills.** Best frames in sequence for a listing or portfolio; strong first image.
- **Educational "how we shot this."** BTS of a technique or gear choice. Positions expertise; feeds [[10x-ig-repositioning]].
- **Listing showcase.** Structured tour toward the sale — see [[property-film-structure]].

## Retention tactics
- Fast first cut; get to substance immediately.
- Constant motion; kill dead frames and static holds.
- Design for loop-ability — end frame flows back to start.
- Pace on-screen text to reading speed; one idea per card.
- Vary shot length; cut before the eye gets bored.

## Cut to music
- Edit to the track, not the track to the edit. Cut on beats and drops.
- Source and licensing: [[music-library]].

## Format for platform
- 9:16 vertical, full frame.
- Respect safe margins — keep text/subject clear of UI (top, bottom, right rail).
- Set the cover frame deliberately; first frame = thumbnail.

## Graphics discipline
- Text on footage, never on tiles ([[brand-system]]).
- Type in the bottom gradient; never cover the subject.

## CTA discipline
- Organic: soft or no CTA. Let the work talk.
- Ad: explicit DM CTA per the approved formula in [[ig-caption-voice]].
