# Craft — Knowledge Base

The durable *how-to-shoot / how-to-edit / how-to-sell* layer of the wiki: standing reference knowledge for the actual disciplines behind 10x Visuals, separate from the client/ops pages. Business systems live in [[index]]; this is the craft.

Current focus: **[[architectural-photography]]** — the discipline Hunter is actively levelling up.

## Domains

- **[[architectural-photography]]** — shooting buildings, interiors and exteriors: composition, verticals, exposure blending, timing, gear.
- **[[videography]]** — cinematography and property film: camera settings, movement, structure, audio.
- **[[post-production]]** — the edit: photo pipeline, colour grading, video edit and scoring.
- **[[ai-toolkit]]** — AI generation as a craft: which tool for which job, prompting, video models. Routing rules stay in [[higgsfield-pipelines]] and [[staging-rules]].
- **[[content-systems]]** — turning shoots into a feed: reel formats, hooks, cadence, pillars. Voice discipline stays in [[ig-caption-voice]] and [[brand-system]].
- **[[business-craft]]** — the trade around the work: pricing, packaging, onboarding, lead-gen and sales.

## How this layer works

- Reference-first: each page is a standing checklist/recipe Hunter (or an editor, or Claude) can act from without re-deriving it.
- Craft pages describe *technique*; client pages describe *what we did for whom*. When a technique is proven on a job, note it on the client page and link back here.
- Additive to the existing wiki — the AI, brand and voice concept pages remain the source of truth for their rules; craft pages point to them, never duplicate.
