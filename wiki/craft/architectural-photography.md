## Core principles

- Verticals stay vertical. Level the camera; fix the rest in post ([[perspective-correction]]).
- One dominant light source per frame. Don't fight mixed direction; let one win, blend the rest ([[exposure-blending]]).
- Shoot for the edit. Bracket, shoot wide, leave crop room — the file is raw material, not the final.
- Perspective is a decision. One-point (straight-on, symmetrical) reads formal; two-point (three-quarter) shows depth and volume. Choose per subject.
