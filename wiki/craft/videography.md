# Videography

Map of shooting motion for property, construction and architecture films.

## Core principles
- 180-degree shutter rule: shutter ≈ 1/(2×fps) for natural motion blur.
- Shoot flat/log at native ISO for a wide grade in [[color-grading]].
- Motivated movement only — every move reveals, follows or adds parallax. No aimless drift.
- Every clip is cut to the edit and the music. Shoot slightly slow so moves speed up and cut clean.
