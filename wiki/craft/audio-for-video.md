# Audio for Video

## Music-led (default for property)
- Property films are usually music-led, no dialogue — scoring is king.
- Track choice sets pace and tone; cut the edit to the beats. See [[music-library]], [[music-taxonomy]].
- One track, chosen early — the film is built on it, not scored after.

## When there is talent / VO
- Mic choice: lav (hidden, hands-free, close), shotgun (directional boom for controlled setups), on-camera (scratch/reference only).
- Record clean: peaks -12 to -6 dBFS, never clip (digital clip is unrecoverable).
- Always monitor with headphones — you can't fix what you didn't hear.
- Room tone: capture 30s of silence per location for gap-filling and smoothing.

## Field practice
- Wind protection outdoors — deadcat/furry on any exposed mic.
- Sync: scratch audio or a slate/clap when recording dual-system.
- Log takes; note any noise (aircon, traffic) to fix in post.

## Sweetening
- Layer ambient/foley under the music for realism: footsteps, doors, nature, room ambience.
- Sits below the music bed — felt, not heard.

## The mix
- Music bed at a consistent level; duck it under VO/dialogue when present.
- Clean transitions — fades, no hard music cuts unless intentional.
- Master loudness ~-14 LUFS integrated for social (IG/YouTube). True peak ≤ -1 dBTP.
