# Lead Gen and Sales

Finding and converting clients.

## Lead sources

- **Referrals** — highest-trust, lowest-friction. Earn them by over-delivering.
- **Own-brand IG** — 10x's feed as a proof and credibility engine ([[content-systems]]). Your work is your portfolio and your ad.
- **Outbound** — targeted approach to a defined niche (property, construction, architecture, fitness).
- **Inbound from ads** — paid reach when you want volume.

## The credibility loop

Great published work → attracts similar clients → more great work to publish. The feed compounds. Publish the wins.

## Two front doors

- **Low-friction solo retainer** — the easy entry (~$1,500/mo, see [[pricing-and-packaging]]).
- **Agency package** — the upsell for clients ready to scale.

Match the door to the prospect's readiness. Don't pitch the package to a cold, cautious lead.

## Pitch flow

1. **Warm identify** — find a prospect with a live pain (bad incumbent, thin feed).
2. **Tailored proposal** — built in the recipient's world, per [[proposal-playbook]].
3. **Incumbent comparison** — turn their existing frustration into the pitch.
4. **Undercut + guarantee** — visible saving, shortfall-carry-over guarantee.
5. **Close** — 3-bullet getting-started, low-risk term.

## Qualifying

- Budget — can they sustain a retainer?
- Fit — property / construction / architecture / fitness verticals.
- Decision-maker — are you talking to the person who signs off?

## Following up

Warm replies decay fast. Answer with a fast, specific follow-up — reference their exact situation, not a template. The [[anthony]] / Hattie Boydle thread is the worked example: a warm reply converted through a tailored proposal, not a generic deck.

## Lead-gen ads as a service

Running lead-gen ADS *for* clients is itself a 10x package — sell the pipeline you built for yourself.

**⚠️ Sell it from a funnel that works.** 10xvisuals.com.au carries "Lead Gen" as a nav-level service while having no lead form of its own (verified 27 Jul 2026), and the 20 Jul investment-leads Meta campaign spent ~$160 for zero leads. Fix the shoemaker's shoes before pitching the package.

**The one proven pattern:** [[strategic-investing-qld]] converted 10–15 leads in 4 business days. Copy that recipe rather than inventing per client — but confirm its attribution first (two assets shipped, one of them CTA-free by design).

**Diagnose in two buckets, never one:** *no leads* = channel / offer / capture broken. *Leads don't convert* = follow-up speed, qualification, offer mismatch. A client slow to call back cannot be fixed with better ads. As of 27 Jul we have no speed-to-contact data for any client — that gap is the next thing to close.

Track the pipeline in the CRM — the source of truth for lead status, not scattered files or chat. Every prospect has a stage.
