# Photo Gear & Settings

Kit and baseline settings for architectural work.

## Body

- **Full-frame mirrorless** — the dynamic range matters for **window pulls** and clean shadow recovery in blends.

## Lenses

- **Wide zoom 16–35 mm** — the workhorse for interiors and exteriors.
- **Tilt-shift** — the pro tool for straight verticals via rise (shoot tall buildings level). See [[perspective-correction]].
- **Standard / short tele** (35–85 mm) — detail shots, materials, compression on facades.
- Avoid over-using the widest end indoors — distortion lies about room size.

## Support

- **Sturdy tripod** — non-negotiable for bracketing and blends.
- **Geared / leveling head** — fine, precise framing and fast leveling.
- **Bubble level + electronic level** — level side-to-side AND front-to-back.

## Triggering

- **Remote or 2s timer** and **electronic front-curtain shutter** to kill vibration.
- **AEB** (auto exposure bracketing) for fast, consistent brackets.

## Baseline settings

- **RAW**, always.
- **ISO 100** (base ISO) for cleanest files.
- **f/8–f/11** for depth and lens sweet spot.
- **Single-point focus**, ~a third into the room; then don't refocus across the bracket.
- **Manual or fixed WB** — never auto across a bracket.
- **Bracket** (3–7 frames, 2 EV apart), vary shutter only. See [[exposure-blending]].

## Filters

- **Polariser** — cuts glare on glass, water, and reflective surfaces; deepens sky and foliage. Careful on **ultrawide** — uneven sky darkening across the frame.
- **ND** — long exposures (smooth water/cloud) and the video crossover.

## Discipline

- **Card + backup** — dual cards or offload on-site; format in-camera; never reuse an un-backed card.
