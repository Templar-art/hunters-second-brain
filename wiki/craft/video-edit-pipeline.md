# Video Edit Pipeline

Property / social video, ingest to delivery. Resolve or Premiere.

## 1. Ingest & organise
- Offload + backup (3-2-1). Generate proxies for 4K/log for smooth cutting.
- Bins by scene/type: drone, interior, exterior, detail, interview.
- Sync external audio to camera (waveform/timecode).

## 2. Select
- Watch all takes, mark selects. Stringout the best moments per angle. Kill the rest.

## 3. Assembly
- Build to structure — [[property-film-structure]] (hook → establish → walk-through → feature beats → resolve/CTA).
- Rough cut for story and length before polish.

## 4. Cut to music
- Pick the track first — [[music-library]] / [[music-taxonomy]]. The song sets pace and length.
- Cut on the beat. Let energy build drive escalation. Land key reveals on musical hits.

## 5. Pacing for platform
- Social/reels: fast hook in the first 1-2s, quick cuts, retention-driven.
- Brand film: let it breathe, longer holds, considered rhythm.

## 6. Transitions
- Mostly hard cuts. Speed ramps and match cuts for motion. Minimal effects.
- Restraint on graphics — [[brand-system]] "nothing moves". No flashy wipes.

## 7. Titles / graphics
- On-brand type only: address, agency/builder, logo lockup. Clean, static, minimal.

## 8. Colour
- Grade to one consistent look — [[color-grading]]. Match all sources.

## 9. Audio mix
- [[audio-for-video]]: music level, dialogue clarity, ambience/SFX, loudness normalise (~-14 LUFS social).

## 10. Export
- 16:9 master + 9:16 vertical ([[reel-formats]]).
- H.264/H.265 high bitrate, correct fps (match project), rec.709.
- Per-platform loudness and dimensions.

## 11. Versioning & review
- Frame.io-style review link. One round of consolidated notes, then lock.
- Name versions clearly (v01, v02_final).

## Handoff
- To/from [[lachlan]]: shared project, consistent bins, locked structure and music before fine cut. Brief the look and deliverables up front.
