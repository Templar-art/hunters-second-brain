# Property Film Structure

## The standard arc
Establish → approach → interior journey (room-by-room) → hero features → lifestyle/detail → resolve (pull-out).

- Establish: exterior wide / drone setting.
- Approach: move toward and through the entry.
- Interior journey: logical room order, follow the natural walk-through.
- Hero features: kitchen, void, pool, view — the reasons the property sells.
- Lifestyle/detail: texture inserts, human moments, light play.
- Resolve: pull-out / drone-away to close the loop.

## Hook
- First 1–3 seconds must stop the scroll — strongest move or best reveal up front for social.
- Don't save the best shot for the end on a reel; front-load it.

## Coverage checklist
- Wide establishing (exterior + key interiors).
- Mediums (rooms, transitions).
- Detail/texture inserts (materials, fittings, joinery, light).
- Movement variety (push, orbit, slide, reveal, locked-off).
- Lifestyle (people using the space, ambience).

## Cut to length & music
- 15–30s reel — social, tight, hype or luxury.
- 60–90s walkthrough — full property tour.
- 2–3min brand film — story-led, client voice.
- Cut to music beats; pick tier from [[music-taxonomy]] → Cinematic/luxury for premium, Hype/social for reels ([[music-library]]).

## Rules
- B-roll variety — no two adjacent shots the same move, focal length or subject.
- Match client voice and brand — [[brand-system]], [[ig-caption-voice]].
- Continuity — consistent light direction and time of day within a sequence; shoot golden features at the right hour ([[light-and-timing]]).

## Deliverables
- 16:9 master + 9:16 vertical for reels.
- Frame safe for both crops — protect the vertical center; leave headroom.
