# Matterport & 3D Tours

## What it is
A navigable 3D walkthrough of a property. Buyers "walk" room to room online, plus:
- **Dollhouse** view (whole home rotated in 3D).
- **Floor-plan / schematic** view.
- Sometimes measurements and tags.
Lets remote and interstate buyers self-tour before booking an inspection.

## Platforms & gear
- **Matterport** — the category leader. Pro3/Pro2 cameras, or supported 360 cameras (Insta360, Ricoh Theta) driven via the Matterport app. Subscription hosting.
- **Alternatives** — Zillow 3D Home, CloudPano, iGUIDE (iGUIDE also emphasises accurate floor plans/measurements).
- Tools and pricing change — stay tool-agnostic; sell the outcome, not the brand.

## Capture workflow
- Tripod at a consistent height (~chest/eye level) for a natural walk feel.
- Scan positions every ~1.5–2.5 m and one **through each doorway** so the tour links rooms cleanly.
- Consistent lighting: lights ON, blinds set as for stills, same look throughout.
- Avoid mirrors (they confuse the depth sensor), moving people, and pets.
- **Stay out of frame** — step behind the camera / into an already-scanned area between scans.
- Scan the whole property including outdoor/entry where relevant; close loops so navigation is smooth.

## Where it fits
Separate from and **complementary to** hero stills and video — not a replacement. Stills sell the emotion; the 3D tour sells the layout and confidence. See [[real-estate-vs-architectural]] and [[deliverables-and-export]].

## Deliverables
- Hosted tour link + embed code for the listing.
- Dollhouse view.
- Schematic floor plan.
- Sometimes room measurements.

## Packaging & pricing
Sell as an **add-on** to a stills/video package, not the core. Recurring hosting cost passes through or gets bundled. Adds meaningful time on site vs stills alone — quote the extra scan time. See [[business-craft]] and [[shot-list-and-planning]].
