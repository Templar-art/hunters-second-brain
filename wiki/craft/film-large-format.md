# Film & Large Format

## The lineage
Large-format **view cameras** were THE architectural tool. Full front and rear movements give total control over perspective and the focus plane — exactly what architecture demands. Everything digital architectural technique does is descended from this.

## View camera movements
- **Rise / fall / shift** — move the lens (or back) parallel to the film to reframe without tilting the camera. Keeps verticals vertical. This is the origin of what [[tilt-shift-technique]] emulates on digital, and what [[perspective-correction]] fixes after the fact.
- **Tilt / swing** — angle the lens plane relative to the film to control the plane of focus (**Scheimpflug**): get a whole receding facade or floor sharp front-to-back at a moderate aperture.
- Understanding movements optically sharpens your digital technique — you shift for perspective, tilt for focus, and know why.

## Why it still matters
- **Movements** — physical, precise, seen through the lens before capture.
- **Resolution & tonality** — a 4x5 or 8x10 negative holds enormous detail and smooth tonal transitions.
- **The slow, deliberate process** forces better seeing: composing on ground glass, one considered frame.
- **The film look** — medium/large-format film has a signature that reads as premium for editorial, fine-art, and brand work.

## Formats today
- **4x5 / 8x10 sheet film** view cameras — full movements, the classic path.
- **Medium-format film** — more portable, still a distinct look, movements via specialist bodies/lenses.
- **Instant / large-format** as a craft and marketing angle — behind-the-scenes and differentiation.

## Shooting film for architecture now
- Meter carefully — no chimping; spot-meter shadows and highlights, place tones deliberately. See [[metering-and-dynamic-range]].
- Bracket sparingly — every frame costs money.
- Scan negatives for a hybrid workflow (film capture, digital grade/delivery).
- Slower means **fewer, more considered frames** — the point, not a limitation.

## Honest positioning
A craft-enrichment, high-end-editorial, brand-differentiator path — not a volume tool. It won't shoot a 30-photo real-estate listing on a deadline. But mastering movements here is exactly why a **digital tilt-shift is worth mastering** for everyday work.
