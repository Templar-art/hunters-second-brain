# Business Craft

The trade *around* the work — how 10x sells, prices, and runs client relationships.

The proposal RECIPE (how to build and deliver a winning pitch document) lives in [[proposal-playbook]]. This domain is the wider business craft: the go-to-market model, pricing logic, onboarding, and the sales pipeline that a proposal is only one step inside.

## Two-tier go-to-market

- **Front door — Hunter solo retainer.** ~$1,500/mo all-in, low-friction entry that visibly undercuts incumbents. Direct line to the person filming, fixed deliverables, easy yes.
- **Upsell — 10x Visuals agency package.** Marketing videos, photos, account management, lead-gen ads. Held back as the growth path once the relationship is proven and trust is earned.

Land with the solo retainer; expand into the agency package. Compounding relationship, not one-off jobs.
