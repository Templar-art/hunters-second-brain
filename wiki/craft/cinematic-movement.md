# Cinematic Movement

## Motivated movement
- Every move has a reason: reveal, follow, or parallax. If you can't name it, lock off.
- Motion should lead the eye to the subject, not announce the operator.

## Gimbal fundamentals
- Balance first — a balanced gimbal holds itself level with motors off. Do it every lens/config.
- Walk heel-toe ("ninja walk"): bent knees, glide, absorb steps in the legs.
- Low mode for ground-level pushes and reveals; both hands for stability.
- Pan and tilt slow — the gimbal's job is smoothness, yours is restraint.

## Core moves
- Push-in / pull-out — build or release toward/away from a subject.
- Reveal past foreground — hide then expose the subject behind a wall, plant, doorway.
- Orbit — arc around a hero feature or the whole building for dimensionality.
- Parallax slide — lateral move with a close foreground for depth.
- Follow — track a person or leading line through a space.
- Tilt-reveal up a facade — base to roofline to sell scale.
- Top-down drone reveal — descend or rise to expose the property in context.

## Slider / dolly
- Slider for controlled interiors: precise, repeatable parallax on detail and features.
- Motorized slider for slow, even hero moves and timelapse-style pushes.

## Drone for property
- Fly-away reveal — pull back and up off the entry to open the setting.
- Orbit the house — even arc, subject centered, consistent radius.
- Top-down — roof geometry, pool, block layout.
- Low push toward entry — fast, cinematic approach.
- Australia: fly to CASA rules — sub-2kg / licensing, VLOS, <120m AGL, keep clear of people and controlled airspace; ReOC for commercial where required.

## Speed & the edit
- Shoot moves slightly slow so they can be sped up and cut clean in [[video-edit-pipeline]].
- Match move direction across cuts (left-to-right stays left-to-right) for flow.
- Locked-off tripod shots as breathers — the eye needs stillness between moving shots.
