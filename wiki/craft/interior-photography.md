# Interior Photography

Shooting rooms clean, bright, and true to size.

## Pre-shoot styling / declutter checklist

- Surfaces **clear** — benches, tables, vanities. Minimal, intentional props only.
- **Blinds/curtains even** and consistent height across the room and between windows.
- **Toilet lids down.** Bathmats straight, towels folded.
- **Cords hidden**, chargers away, remotes stowed, bins removed from frame.
- **All lights ON.** Replace mismatched bulbs so colour temperature matches (one look, not tungsten+LED mix).
- Straighten cushions, rugs, chairs, artwork. Fluff and align.

## Perspective per room

- One-point (square-on, symmetrical) for feature walls, symmetrical rooms, hallways.
- Two-point (into the corner) for most rooms — shows two walls and true size. See [[arch-composition]].

## Camera height & level

- **Chest height ~1.2–1.5 m.** Keep the camera **dead level** — verticals vertical. See [[perspective-correction]].

## Lens

- **Wide but not ultra-wide** — ~**16–24 mm** full-frame. Enough to show the room; not so wide it distorts and lies about size.
- Don't **over-stretch small rooms** — ultra-wide makes a bathroom look cavernous and warped; it reads as fake.

## Settings

- Aperture **f/7.1–f/11** for front-to-back depth.
- **ISO low** (base 100). **Tripod** always.
- Single-point focus roughly a third into the room.
- Shoot **RAW**, fixed WB across the bracket.

## Mixed lighting / white balance

- Tungsten + daylight + LED in one room = ugly colour casts.
- **Flambient / blending** solves it: a flash base gives neutral colour, ambient frames restore window views and warmth. See [[exposure-blending]].

## Windows

- Shoot **toward or across** windows for light and views.
- **Expose for the room**, then **pull the windows** back with a darker exposure masked in. Never leave windows blown white.

## Composing rooms

- **Kitchen** — shoot down the bench/island line; include splashback and a window; clear the counters.
- **Bathroom** — two-point corner; lids down; watch mirror reflections of you and the tripod.
- **Bedroom** — bed made crisp, symmetrical framing or corner; include a window and breathing room.

## Shots per room

- Typically **1–2 hero angles** per room (opposite corners), plus a detail if it earns it. Don't shoot every wall.

## Staging note

- Physical styling first. **AI virtual staging** is the downstream option for empty rooms — see [[staging-rules]].
