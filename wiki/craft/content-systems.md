# Content Systems

Turning shoots into a consistent, on-brand feed. The production/format/cadence craft.

VOICE lives in [[ig-caption-voice]]. DESIGN lives in [[brand-system]]. This domain is neither — it is the machine that turns raw footage into shipped posts on a repeatable schedule.

## Child notes
- [[reel-formats]] — repeatable Reel / short-form archetypes and retention tactics.
- [[posting-cadence]] — planning, batching, pillars, calendar, and shipping consistently.

## Operating beliefs
- Win the first second or lose the scroll. Hook is the whole game.
- Native to each account's voice. A post must feel at home in its own feed, not ours.
- Batch produce. Set up once, capture many.
- Every shoot feeds multiple posts. One asset, many formats, many accounts.
- Premium restraint on graphics. Less design = more premium ([[brand-system]]).
