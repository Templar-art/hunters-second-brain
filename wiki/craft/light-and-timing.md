# Light & Timing

Planning the shoot around light. Gold Coast, southern hemisphere.

## The light windows

- **Golden hour** — warm, low, raking light just after sunrise / before sunset. Best for **exterior texture** — rakes across facades, materials, and landscaping to reveal form and depth.
- **Blue hour / twilight** — the **premium exterior look**. ~15–25 min window just after sunset (and before sunrise). Interior and landscape lights ON, sky deep saturated blue, ambient and artificial light balance. This is the money shot for high-end property.
- **Overcast** — soft, even, shadowless. Great for interiors, detail shots, and any facade in ugly harsh sun. A big natural softbox.

## Southern-hemisphere sun path (Gold Coast)

- Sun rises **E**, arcs through the **NORTH**, sets **W**. (Opposite the northern hemisphere.)
- **North-facing facades** get sun most of the day → good for daytime exteriors.
- **South-facing facades** stay largely shaded → use overcast, or shoot at twilight, or light them.
- East facades = morning; west facades = afternoon/sunset.
- Account for this in every scheduling call — don't apply northern-hemisphere assumptions.

## Planning tools

- **PhotoPills** or **Sun Surveyor** — sun/shadow position for any date and time, AR overlay on the actual site.
- Weather/cloud apps for **cloud cover** (clear = golden hour; broken high cloud = dramatic skies; thin overcast = soft).
- Check the exact **sunset time** and be on-site well before it for twilight.

## Scheduling by orientation

- Determine facade orientation first, then pick the window: north facade daytime, west facade golden/sunset, south facade overcast or twilight.
- Interiors — shoot each room when **its windows are NOT in harsh direct sun** (blown views, hard shafts). Rotate the room order to follow the shade.

## Twilight exterior workflow

- Arrive early, compose and lock the tripod in daylight while you can see.
- Turn every interior/exterior light ON.
- As light drops, shoot a **continuous bracket sequence** — the sky changes fast.
- Composite later: ambient sky + window/interior glow, pulling the best sky and the best light balance from across the sequence. See [[exposure-blending]].
