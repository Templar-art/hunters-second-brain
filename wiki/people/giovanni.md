# Giovanni

**Role:** being trained on AI property stills + video for [[overview|10x Visuals]] (onboarding started 28 Jul 2026).

**The brief he was given:** [SOP-AI-PROPERTY-VIDEO.md](../../projects/giovanni-onboarding/SOP-AI-PROPERTY-VIDEO.md) — Hunter's own words, edited by him directly, written in deliberately plain English for someone who has not done this before.

## What the SOP asks for

Take an architect's flat grey render of a house that does not exist yet, make it read as a real photograph of a real room, then make that photograph move for 3–5 seconds and cut the clips together. The reference is Vision Studios / Vision Cinema Homes (`visioncinemahomes.com`), and **phase one is research, not production** — watch their whole feed and take notes on why the shots work before touching a tool. Hunter's framing: that part is the most valuable, and the rest is mostly repetition.

The hard rule is the one this vault already lives by — **the building must stay exactly as the architect drew it.** Light, realism and camera move are ours to change; a window that shifts or a benchtop that changes shape kills the image no matter how good it looks. That is [[staging-rules|preserve fixtures]] arriving in a training doc for a new person, which is the right sign that the rule is real and not just a prompt habit.

Closing instruction worth keeping as a general training pattern: **if something fails twice, stop and send it over — do not try a third time.** Also flagged as always-ask cases: drone shots, wide exteriors, real photos rather than renders, and many generations against one image (the cost gate, in the plainest possible terms).
