# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences for 10x Visuals' own-brand surfaces (@10xvisuals.co + collateral):
- **Prospects** — Gold Coast/SEQ real-estate agents, builders, developers, hospitality operators who land on the profile from a caption credit on a client's post. They decide in ~10 seconds whether 10x looks like the premium option.
- **Clients** — existing accounts (Zed, Salisbury, CLC, Belle, CVA, Jessica Wallace…) whose referrals depend on 10x's grid looking better than what they could get elsewhere.

## Product Purpose

The grid IS the product demo. 10x sells premium film/photo/marketing for property and construction brands; every own-brand graphic must look like the work 10x charges for. Success = a prospect clicking through from a client credit and concluding "these are the expensive ones" (the JUNE/AC Media referral-validation model — coherence over follower count).

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, understated, confident. A film house, not a content shop. Copy is blokey-Aussie-but-not-slangy; graphics say almost nothing and let the footage speak.

## Anti-references

- The 52-tile editorial arsenal look (tiles, bands, boxed layouts) — rejected for the grid ("those graphics are shit"), demoted to ads/utility only.
- CLC or any client playbook ported 1:1 — 10x derives from film/media-house references, not builder accounts.
- Canva-template real-estate marketing (badge overlays, starburst CTAs, agent headshot circles).
- AI-shop positioning — AI is one service line, never the headline.

## Design Principles

1. **The less graphic design on the grid, the more premium it reads** (verified across The Local Project 1.7M, AC Media 64.6K, JUNE). Type on footage, never tiles.
2. **Does this look like the work we charge for?** — the ship/kill question for every asset.
3. **One locked system** — every format shares the same type, scrim, and grammar so the profile reads as a single film house.
4. **Photo-first, clean frames only** — real shoot frames, no third-party branding/pins/watermarks, never already-published media.
5. **Named-work discipline** — every project gets its title treated like a film title ("THE BOWSPRIT."), building a named portfolio over time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

White type must survive its background: adaptive scrim on bright frames, soft drop shadow always. No text in IG UI dead zones (story top ~170px / bottom ~280px). Copy in sentence-length fragments readable at feed scale.
