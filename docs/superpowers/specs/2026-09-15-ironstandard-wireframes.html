Carried across all three pages

  • One stylesheet, one script. assets/css/style.css and assets/js/main.js grow rather than fork. A design change stays a one-file edit.
  • Header and footer are hand-duplicated. At three pages that beats introducing a build step, but it will drift — partials/footer.html already has. Worth revisiting past five pages.
  • Intro sequence plays once per session, on whichever page they land on first. Clicking between pages goes straight in. A new tab plays it again.
  • Existing design language throughout: the #F4502A accent, TASA Orbiter, black-and-white photography, the scroll-reveal already built. Nothing new invented.
  • The WordPress widget route retires once this lands. Two deliverables maintained in parallel is how they drift apart.

Not in this build, and why

  • Pricing or membership tiers — Anthony hasn’t set them.
  • Address or map — location isn’t public yet.
  • Opening date — not confirmed. The site still says “Coming soon”.
  • Timetable or class booking — nothing to schedule before the doors open.
  • Coach bios or an About page — we have “founded by a competitive powerlifter” and no coach lineup. An About page built on that would be padding, and padding is what makes a site read like the budget chains this one is meant to outclass.
Blocked One dependency sits outside the build. No sign-ups are being collected anywhere today — there is no form on the site and no Brevo list. Until that exists, every waitlist button on all three pages is decoration. Worth saying plainly to Anthony rather than describing the dashboard he’ll eventually get.
