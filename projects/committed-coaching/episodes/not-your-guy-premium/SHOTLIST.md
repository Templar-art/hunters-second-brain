# B-roll shot list for Jackson ("Don't work with me" Reel)

**Two short clips, about 10 minutes of filming.** H is the priority. Each plays for 1–2 seconds over your voice, so film **5–8 seconds of each and hold it steady**. We pick the best moment.

**How to film (both clips):**
- **Phone:** vertical, 4K at 50fps if your phone offers it, otherwise 1080p.
- **Camera:** on a tripod or braced, with slow movement only.
- **Light:** bright, even daylight or a well-lit gym, for one look across everything. The talking-head video is bright and clean, so the B-roll should match. No dark or moody gyms.
- **Framing:** keep the action in the **top two-thirds** of the frame. The bottom third should be plain wall, floor or bench, because the captions sit there.
- **Clothes:** the black COMMITTED tee, the same as the talking-head video.
- **Audio:** no talking needed.
- **Faces:** keep clients' faces out of shot unless they've signed a release to appear in an ad.
