# Fitness creative direction

## Coach authority
Goal: explain a useful coaching argument clearly. Specific hook → reasoning → relevant illustration → qualified takeaway. Face-led, semantic captions, sparse useful diagrams. No incessant jump zooms or graphics repeating every word.

## Training/performance
Goal: convey effort, personality and progression. Movement continuity, shot variety, controlled rhythmic sections and natural gym sound. Speed ramps/flash effects are optional, never substitutes for shot selection. Do not misrepresent exercise form or performance.

## Offer/proof
Goal: communicate service, credible evidence and one next action. Real permissioned quote/screenshots, concise process and clear offer hierarchy. No invented body results, fake UI notifications, scarcity or guarantees.

## Brief before code
Audience, objective, main message, content mode, real source ranges, approved claims, brand, energy curve, graphic purposes, reference principles, exclusions, delivery specs and approval checkpoint. Do not infer goals, ability or identity from appearance.

## Style frames
Opening, explanatory midpoint and end treatment. Use actual long text and protect the face/movement regions. One dominant reading task. Approve a coherent treatment before a complicated render.

## Proposed design defaults
One licensed primary font; limited weights; brand neutrals plus one purposeful accent; semantic line breaks; stable captions. At 1080×1920, test roughly 52–68px captions and 88–136px headings, but judge real font/crop/phone-size output. These are hypotheses, not platform requirements. Platform safe zones vary: verify the actual placement. Springs/bounce/glass/grain are choices, not quality signals.

## Attention and restraint
Every graphic must introduce, direct, explain, compare or conclude. If it has no communicative role, omit it. Let important ideas hold. Do not cover the face because the lower third is busy; recompose, simplify or use a cutaway.
