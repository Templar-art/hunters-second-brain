# Annotated reference library

Source-grounded case-study analysis carried forward into this handoff, not frame-by-frame film analysis. No reference-film playback/audio inspection was performed. All adaptations are original proposals, and no exact easing, fonts, timings or motion observations are claimed. Verify current pages before further implementation. No third-party assets/licences are bundled.

## R01 — Peloton — 2021 Cooldown
Source: https://www.stinkstudios.com/work/peloton-cooldown-2021

Documented basis: Studio describes dynamic assembly of relevant scenes according to member activity.

Proposed adaptation: Use truthful scene eligibility and meaningful personalisation; omit unsupported metric scenes.
