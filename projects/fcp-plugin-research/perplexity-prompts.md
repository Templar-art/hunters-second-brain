## Prompt 1: Final Cut Pro plugins that would save us time

> I'm a videographer at a small Australian real-estate and brand video studio. I edit in **Final Cut Pro 12.4 on an Apple M5 Pro MacBook (macOS 27)**. Everything I shoot is on **Sony a7S III and a7R V**, with video in **S-Log3** and some DJI drone footage. What we make:
> - property films and listing videos for real-estate agents (16:9 master, plus 9:16 cuts for Reels and TikTok)
> - talking-head series for business clients, with captions, lower thirds and pop-up graphics
> - short social ads and brand reels for builders, gyms and e-commerce
> - occasional AI-generated video (Kling, Veo, Seedance) cut together with real footage
>
> Plugins I **already have**:
> - **motionVFX:** mCaptions, mTracker 3D, mRotoAI, mFilmLook, mFlare 2, mPuppet, DesignStudio and Freebies
> - **Pixel Film Studios:** Stabilizer, Shape Tracker and Timelines
> - **Ripple Training / RN:** Shake Transitions, Mask Glow, the Effects Expansion Pack and some SFX packs
> - **Others:** Smooth Camera Zooms Pro, Halation, an adjustment layer and assorted Envato title and slideshow templates
>
> Recommend the Final Cut Pro plugins, FxPlug effects, workflow extensions and companion apps that would save the most editing time, or noticeably raise quality, for this kind of work. Cover these areas, and say which ones matter most:
> 1. **Audio:** dialogue cleanup and denoise, auto-ducking music under voice, loudness normalising to −14 LUFS for social and −23 for broadcast, and SFX libraries that live inside FCP.
> 2. **Captions and text:** auto-captions with good styling, burned-in word-by-word social captions, and translation.
> 3. **Reframing:** 16:9 to 9:16 with subject tracking. How do the plugins compare with FCP's built-in Smart Conform?
> 4. **Colour:** S-Log3 to Rec.709 conversion LUTs (Sony's official LUT vs paid packs), colour-managed workflows in FCP, matching drone footage to Sony, and grading plugins such as Color Finale and Dehancer.
> 5. **Speed and organisation:** auto-transcription and text-based editing, finding selects, keyword and metadata tools, and proxy and render management.
> 6. **Motion graphics:** clean, minimal real-estate title and price templates, map and location animations, and logo reveals.
> 7. **AI tools that work inside FCP:** cleanup, upscaling, object removal, and B-roll search.
> 8. **Interchange:** moving projects between FCP and DaVinci Resolve (for grading) and Frame.io for client review.
>
> For each pick, give me:
> - the developer
> - the current price in AUD or USD, and whether it's a one-off purchase or a subscription
> - whether it runs natively on Apple silicon and has been confirmed working on FCP 12.x
> - what it replaces or speeds up
> - an honest weakness
>
> Flag anything that overlaps with what I already own, and anything FCP 12 now does natively, so I don't pay twice. Finish with a ranked "buy first" shortlist of 5–8 items, with a total cost, and a "skip" list. Only use sources from 2025–2026, and cite them.

## Prompt 2: Better sound effects for video edits

> I make real-estate and brand videos in Final Cut Pro. I need better sound effects for transitions, title pops, whooshes, risers, impacts, UI clicks for talking-head graphics, and subtle ambience. Synthesised SFX I've generated have sounded cheap. I already own the **Ocular Sounds** libraries (Foundation, Pendulum, Aperture, Rainforest) and some Ripple Training SFX packs. I also use a local Web Audio synth tool, React Bits Sound Lab, for small UI sounds.
>
> Research the following:
> 1. **Libraries:** the best-value SFX libraries for this style in 2025–2026. Cover subscriptions (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Soundly, Envato, Splice) and one-off packs (Boom Library, Sonniss GDC bundles, SoundMorph, Cinematic Sound Design). Is Soundly, used as an SFX search tool with an FCP workflow, worth it?
> 2. **Licensing:** which options are cleared for commercial client work and paid social ads with no extra licence, and which aren't.
> 3. **Sound design technique:** how pros make synthesised or library SFX sound expensive. Cover layering (transient plus body plus tail), pitch and time variation, matching reverb to the scene, sidechaining under music, and LUFS targets for SFX within a −14 LUFS social mix.
> 4. **Free or cheap tools:** for designing custom SFX on a Mac, compare Logic Pro's built-in tools, Vital, Krotos Studio, Tonsturm Whoosh, Sound Particles and AI SFX generators (ElevenLabs SFX, Stable Audio, Adobe). Cover the quality, cost and licence of each.
> 5. **Workflow:** an efficient way to audition and drop SFX on the beat or on the cut in Final Cut Pro, including any plugins or extensions that put an SFX browser inside FCP.
>
> Give concrete product names, prices (AUD or USD), licence terms and a recommended starter setup under about AUD $300 a year. Cite your sources.
