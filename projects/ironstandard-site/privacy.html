01Who we are

This policy explains how Iron Standard, a strength and performance training facility in Oslo, Norway, collects and uses personal data through this website. Iron Standard is the data controller for the personal data described here.

We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as it applies in Norway through the Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven).

02What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the enquiry form:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the type of enquiry you select
  • anything you write in your message

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, sensitive information such as health details through the form. If an enquiry later needs that kind of information, for example for physiotherapy, it is handled directly and separately.

03How we use it

We use your details to:

  • read and answer your enquiry
  • pass it to the right person, such as a coach, trainer or physiotherapist
  • follow up on that same enquiry

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering a question you have asked us, and, where you ask about membership or a service, taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement.

We never sell your data, and we do not add you to a mailing list or send marketing without your separate consent.

04Who we share it with

Enquiries are stored in a Google Workspace account controlled by Iron Standard, so Google acts as our data processor. The company hosting this website also processes basic technical data, such as IP addresses in server logs, to deliver the site securely.

Where a processor is located outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard. We share enquiries with nobody else unless the law requires it.

05How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need to answer it and follow up. If it does not lead to a membership or a service, we delete it within 12 months of our last contact with you.

If you become a member, your details move into the membership terms you are given at that point.

06Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • object to or restrict how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable format

To use any of these rights, send a request through the contact form. We answer within one month.

07Cookies and similar technology

This website does not use cookies. It sets no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies and stores nothing on your device.

The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch the font, which means Google receives your IP address. Google states that it does not use this information to profile visitors.

If analytics or any other cookies are introduced in future, this section will list exactly what is set and why, and nothing that is not strictly necessary will be set without your consent.

08Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need them to answer you.

09Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what we collect or how we use it changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

10Contact and complaints

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the contact form.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, at datatilsynet.no.
