10Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, which is also the standard behind Norway's universal design rules.

In practice that means:

  • text contrast that meets or exceeds AA throughout, including black labels on orange buttons
  • every page works with a keyboard alone, with a clear focus outline
  • images have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers
  • text resizes with your browser and device settings, up to 200 percent, without breaking the layout
  • if your device is set to reduce motion, scroll animations switch off and every page shows as a static layout

If you find something hard to use, please tell us through the contact form and we will fix it. You can also contact the Norwegian Authority for Universal Design of ICT (Tilsynet for universell utforming av ikt) at uutilsynet.no.
