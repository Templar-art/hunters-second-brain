FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

If something is not confirmed yet, this page says so rather than guessing.

The facility

Where is the facility?

The location is central Oslo. The exact address is published once the fit-out is confirmed.

When does it open?

The opening date is announced once the build schedule is locked. Enquiries made now are answered in order.

How big is it?

The facility is 800 m², purpose-built and laid out for training rather than for membership density.

Is access really 24/7?

Yes. Members have genuine round-the-clock access rather than extended opening hours.

Training here

Who is this for?

People who train consistently to a programme, and athletes competing in strength or physique sport. The standard is high, the culture is not hostile.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. The requirement is intent rather than a particular level. Coaching is available if you want structure from the start.

Is it a men's gym?

No. Men and women train here on equal terms and are represented equally in everything we publish.

What equipment is there?

Competition platforms, calibrated plates, specialist strength and physique machines and a dedicated posing room. The full list is published as procurement is locked.

Coaching and services

Do you offer coaching?

Yes. One-to-one, semi-private and small group coaching are all available, arranged individually after an enquiry.

Can I train my own clients there?

Independent trainers are welcome. Trainer enquiries are handled separately from member enquiries.

Is physiotherapy available on site?

On-site physiotherapy is planned as part of the facility so that rehabilitation and programming stay connected.

How do I get in touch?

Use the contact page and choose the type of enquiry. It is routed to the person best placed to answer it.

Answers are updated as the opening date, location and membership details are confirmed.
