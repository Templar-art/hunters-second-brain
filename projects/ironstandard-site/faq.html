The facility

Where is the facility?

The facility is in Oslo. The exact address is published once the fit-out is confirmed.

When does it open?

The opening date is announced once the build schedule is locked. Enquiries made now are answered in order.

How big is it?

The facility is 800 m², purpose-built, with the floor given to training instead of to membership numbers.

Is access really 24/7?

Yes. Members have genuine round-the-clock access, not simply long opening hours.

Training here

Who is this for?

People who train consistently to a programme, and athletes competing in strength or physique sport. The standard is high and it applies to everyone the same way.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. What matters is that you train with intent. Coaching is there if you want structure from the start.

Is it a men's gym?

No. Men and women train here on the same floor, to the same standard, and appear in equal number in everything we publish.

What equipment is there?

Competition platforms, calibrated plates, specialist strength and physique machines and a dedicated posing room. The full list is published as procurement is locked.

Coaching and services

Do you offer coaching?

Yes. One-to-one, semi-private and small group coaching, online programming and nutrition support are all arranged individually after an enquiry. See Coaching for how each works.

Can I train my own clients there?

Independent trainers are welcome. Trainer enquiries are handled separately from member enquiries.

Is physiotherapy available on site?

On-site physiotherapy is planned as part of the facility so that rehabilitation and programming stay connected.

How do I get in touch?

Use the contact page and choose the type of enquiry. It is routed to the person best placed to answer it.

Answers are updated as the opening date, location and membership details are confirmed.
