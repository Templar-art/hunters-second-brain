Coaching

Expertise on the floor

Coaching, independent trainers and physiotherapy operate inside the same building as the training floor.

01What is offered

One-to-one coaching

Direct work with a coach for lifters with a competition date, a lift that has stalled, or a plan that needs writing properly. Sessions are programmed around the athlete rather than around a template.

Semi-private and small group

Programmed training in small numbers with a coach on the floor rather than a class taken from the front. Useful for lifters who want structure and supervision without full one-to-one cost.

Independent trainers

Facilities for self-employed coaches who want a professional room and an aligned member base. Trainer enquiries are handled separately from member enquiries so each is answered by the right person.

On-site physiotherapy

Assessment, rehabilitation and performance support from practitioners who understand loading. Working in the same building as the training floor means rehabilitation and programming stay connected.

02How it works

Start with a conversation

Every coaching relationship starts with an enquiry rather than a sign-up form. Tell us what you train for and what you are trying to fix, and the enquiry goes to the person best placed to answer it.

Programming, nutrition support and competition preparation are arranged individually. Nothing here is sold as a package before the requirement is understood.

Individual coach and practitioner profiles will be published as the team is confirmed.
