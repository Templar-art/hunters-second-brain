01The position

For people in Oslo who take strength and performance training seriously, Iron Standard is the purpose-built facility that provides the equipment, space and access commercial gyms cannot.

The standard is high and it applies to everyone equally. Commitment counts here, image does not, and the room belongs to anyone willing to meet that standard.

03Who trains here

Trained with intent

People who train several times a week to a programme, and athletes competing or preparing to. Men and women, in equal number, wanting the same three things: equipment that holds up, space that is actually free when you need it, and a room that takes the work seriously.

Men and women train here on the same floor, to the same standard.
