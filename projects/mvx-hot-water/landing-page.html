Quick answers

How fast can you get to me?

We're based on the Gold Coast and treat no hot water as urgent. Most repair calls get a same day response. Call 0427 593 990 and we'll give you an honest ETA on the spot, not a four hour window.

My hot water stopped completely. Is that expensive?

Usually not. A dead element or a tripped thermostat is one of the cheapest jobs we do, and it's the most common cause. We diagnose before we quote, so you're not paying for a new system you didn't need.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

Rough rule: if the tank itself is leaking, replacement is the only real option, because the cylinder has corroded through. If it's electrical and the tank is sound, repair almost always wins. Over about 10 to 12 years old, a heat pump upgrade often pays for itself in running costs. We'll tell you which one you're looking at before we quote.

Do I need an electrician or a plumber?

Usually both, and that's the annoying part. The electrical side, the element, thermostat, circuit, isolator, switchboard and compliance certificate, is licensed electrical work and it's where these systems actually fail. Where plumbing work is required it's carried out by a licensed plumber as part of the same job, so you make one call and get one bill.

How much is the heat pump rebate really worth?

Eligible heat pumps attract federal small-scale technology certificates, generally applied as an upfront discount on the install rather than claimed back later. The exact amount depends on the system and the certificate price at the time, so we confirm it when we quote rather than promising a number on a web page.

Do you handle rentals and commercial?

Yes. Investment properties, rentals, offices, shops and facilities across the Gold Coast and Northern NSW. We can deal with the agent or tenant directly and invoice the owner.
