Trace roof

How

Click the corners of each roof section, at the eave line (the roof's outer edge). Click the first point again to close it. Trace each wing as its own section where roofs meet, or the whole roof as one if it is one shape. Patios with a flat or pergola roof go in as "Flat". Then place the numbered markers.
