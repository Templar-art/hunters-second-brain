      Treatment areas

      Belly

      Use the wide head. Slow clockwise circles, then strokes upward toward the ribs. 15 minutes.

      Arms

      Use the small head. Strokes from elbow to armpit along the back of the arm. 7 minutes each side.

      Thighs and hips

      Use the medium head. Long upward strokes from knee to hip, front, outer and back. 7 minutes each leg.
