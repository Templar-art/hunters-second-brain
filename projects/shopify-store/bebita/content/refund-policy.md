## Faulty or not as described
Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law come first. If your order arrives faulty, damaged, or not as described, email us at [EMAIL] with your order number and a photo. We'll repair, replace or refund it, and we cover the return postage. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## Change of mind
Changed your mind? You can return unworn items within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.

- Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached, and in their original packaging.
- **Swimwear:** for hygiene reasons, swimwear can only be returned for change of mind if the hygiene liner is still in place and the item has only been tried on over underwear.
- Return postage is at your cost. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund a return that doesn't reach us.
- Original shipping charges aren't refunded for change-of-mind returns.
- Sale items can be exchanged but not refunded for change of mind.

## Exchanges
Need a different size or colour? Email [EMAIL] with your order number and what you'd like instead. We'll send the new item once your return arrives, subject to stock.

## How to return
1. Email [EMAIL] with your order number and what you're returning.
2. We'll reply with the return address.
3. Once your return arrives and is checked, we refund your original payment method within 5 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
