# Bebita: launch-to-sales plan + ads to film

**Who gets paid:** Hunter. The store, the payouts and the income are his.

**The goal:** sell through 336 pieces (about $17,200 at full price) profitably. Every sale is cash back from stock you already own, so the only real costs are ads, Shopify/payment fees, postage and discounts.

**The rule for ads:** spend no more than $1 in ads for every $3 in sales (a ROAS of 3 or more). Below that, pause and fix the ad or the offer before spending more.

---

## Step 1: get paid (do this before anything else, ~30 min)
1. **Settings → Store details:** change the contact email to a real inbox, not the Apple hidden one.
2. **Settings → Payments → Shopify Payments → Complete setup:** use **your** details (Hunter): choose sole trader and enter your ABN (the 10x Visuals one works if you're a sole trader), plus **your** BSB and account so payouts land with you. Make sure the store owner account on Shopify is you as well (Settings → Users).
3. **Check Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay are on.** This is the sign Shopify Payments worked.
4. **PayPal → Manage:** log in to a real PayPal account.
5. **Test order:** buy the $29 sarong with your own card, check the confirmation email arrives, then refund it in Orders.

## Step 1b: new accounts (you don't have the old ones, ~30 min)
You don't have the old @bebitaactive / @bebitaswim, so start fresh under your own control:
1. **Instagram:** create **@bebitacollective** (it matches the store name; if it's taken, try @bebita.collective or @shopbebita). Use one account for Active and Swim together. Set it as a **Business** account.
2. **Facebook page:** create a "Bebita Collective" page, which Meta needs for ads, and link it to the Instagram.
3. **TikTok (optional but worth it):** the same handle. Post the same videos there for free reach.
4. **Fill the grid before running ads:** post **9–12 posts** (the AI model shots, carousels and shoot-day clips). When ad viewers tap through to an empty profile, it kills trust.
5. Tell me the handle and I'll add it to the website footer.

**Why it matters:** with no old followers, the free launch week will be smaller, so **paid ads do more of the work.** Expect the first sales to come mostly from ads in week 2, not from week 1.

## Step 2: tracking + free sales tools (~1 hour, before any ads)
1. **Sales channels → add "Facebook & Instagram"** and connect your new Facebook page, new Instagram account (Step 1b) and a Meta ad account. This installs the Meta pixel + Conversions API, which ads need to find buyers, and syncs the products as a catalogue.
2. **Turn on Instagram Shopping** in the same channel. Once approved (a few days), tag products in posts and stories so people can tap to buy.
3. **Sales channels → add "Google & YouTube"** and connect a Google account. This sends the products to Google Merchant Center (free listings on Google Shopping straight away) and sets up conversion tracking for Google Ads.
4. **Marketing → Automations:** turn on the **Welcome** email (BEBITA10) and the **Abandoned checkout** email. The text is in `launch-copy.md`. Abandoned checkout alone usually wins back 5–10% of lost carts.
5. **Install Judge.me (free)** for reviews. It emails buyers automatically after delivery to ask for one.

## Step 3: free launch week (no ad spend)
1. Put the store link in the new account's bio.
2. Post the 3 launch posts and the 3-frame story (copy is in `launch-copy.md`).
3. **Post a story every day that week:** a new product, a "last sizes" update, a packing-orders video.
4. **Follow and engage** with Gold Coast gym, pilates and beach accounts, and reply to every comment. You're starting from zero followers, so this week is about getting noticed.
5. **Post on your own accounts** and ask your sister and a few friends with a following to share it.
6. Expect your first sales here. They also "train" the Meta pixel before you pay for ads.

## Step 4: shoot day (Hunter films: one day gets 2–3 weeks of ads)
- Shoot the **ad scripts below**, all **vertical 9:16**, at **15–30 seconds** each.
- Shoot **3 different opening hooks** for each ad (first 2 seconds), so you can test which stops the scroll.
- Get plenty of **back views** of the scrunch and V-back pieces. That's what sells them.
- Natural light or a ring light, clean background, real gym, real beach. Leave plain space at the top for captions.
- Film 10–15 short clips of just the clothes: hangers, folding, flat-lay-to-try-on, the swatch row of colours.

## Step 5: run paid ads (start week 2)
**Meta (Instagram + Facebook):** main channel, starting at **$30/day**
- Campaign: **Advantage+ Sales** (Advantage+ shopping), objective **Sales/Purchase**, Australia, women, 18–40 (let Meta go broad).
- Load **4–6 ads**: 3 videos + 1 carousel + 1 static.
- **Don't touch it for 7 days.** Meta needs about 50 purchase events to learn properly.
- **After 7 days:**
  - **Kill** any ad with a click-through rate under 1%, or that has spent $40 with no sale.
  - **Keep** anything at ROAS 3 or more.
  - **Scale** winners by adding 20% to the budget every 3 days, not all at once.
- **Every week:** swap in 1–2 new ads so they don't go stale.

**Google:** second channel, starting at **$15/day**
- Campaign: **Performance Max** using the product feed from Step 2. Google shows the products in Shopping, YouTube and Gmail.
- Add a small **Search** campaign ($5/day) on searches for the brand name, plus "scrunch bum shorts", "v back leggings" and "matching gym set australia".

**Month 1 budget:** about **$1,000–1,300** in ads. If Meta hits ROAS 3 or more, that's roughly $3,000–4,000 in sales.

## Step 6: weekly check (15 min every Monday)
| Number | Where | Healthy |
|---|---|---|
| Ad click-through rate (link CTR) | Meta Ads Manager | over 1% |
| Cost per click | Meta / Google | under $1.50 |
| Add-to-cart rate | Shopify Analytics | over 6% |
| Conversion rate | Shopify Analytics | over 1.5% |
| ROAS | Meta / Google | over 3 |
| Average order value | Shopify Analytics | aim over $90 (top + bottom) |

**What to fix when a number is bad:**
- **Low CTR:** the ad's opening isn't stopping the scroll, so try a new hook.
- **Good CTR but no add-to-carts:** the product page or price isn't convincing.
- **Add-to-carts but no sales:** check shipping cost and checkout.

**As sizes sell out:**
- Turn off ads for products with only 1–2 sizes left.
- Run "last sizes" stories instead.

## Tax (quick note)
The sales are your income. If you're a sole trader with one ABN, Bebita sales count toward the same turnover as 10x Visuals. Once that total passes $75k a year you have to be registered for GST and charge it. Keep the Shopify payout reports and ask your accountant how to record it.

## Step 7: after the first sales
1. Pack fast and post a "packing orders" story every time. It's free social proof.
2. Repost any customer photos or reviews (ask permission).
3. **When about 70% has sold,** run a final "last call" push (email + stories + ads) so the remaining pieces go.

# Ads to film

These are written for Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories (9:16). Every ad ends on the product and the store link. Only claim what's true: test the squat ad on camera first, and don't run it if the fabric shows through.

### Ad 1: "Build your set" (mix and match)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "Small on top, medium on the bottom? Same."
- **Shots:** model holding up a top in S and shorts in M → quick cuts of the same bra with 4 different bottoms in different colours → final outfit, mirror selfie.
- **On-screen text:** Every piece sold separately · Your size, top and bottom · Mix any colour
- **Caption:** Matching sets that actually fit. Every Bebita top and bottom is sold on its own, so you pick your size in each. Final stock from the original run. 10% off your first order.
- **Headline:** Build your own set

### Ad 2: "The original run is back" (honest scarcity)
- **Hook:** "We found the last of the original Bebita run."
- **Shots:** boxes opening → folded pieces in every colour → hands laying out the colour range → fast try-on montage.
- **On-screen text:** 336 pieces left · No restocks · When it's gone, it's gone
- **Caption:** The original Bebita Active and Swim run is back for the last time. No restocks, so when your size goes, it's gone. Shop now, sent from the Gold Coast.
- **Headline:** Final stock, no restocks
- *Update the number as stock drops. It has to stay true.*

### Ad 3: "The V-back" (back-view hero)
- **Hook:** walking away from camera, then text: "The waistband that does the work."
- **Shots:** slow walk away in the V-Back Scrunch Leggings → close-up of the V dipping at the back → side profile → turns and smiles.
- **On-screen text:** V-back waistband · Scrunch seat · Buttery soft
- **Caption:** The V-Back Scrunch, in leggings ($59) and shorts ($45). The waistband dips into a V at the back, and the scrunch seam does the rest.
- **Headline:** Meet the V-Back

### Ad 4: "Squat test" (only if it passes)
- **Hook:** "Squat test in the brightest light we've got."
- **Shots:** ring light → deep squat in front of the camera → close-up showing no see-through → thumbs up.
- **On-screen text:** Bright light ✓ Deep squat ✓ Not see-through ✓
- **Caption:** We put the leggings through the bright-light squat test so you don't have to. Seamless and performance styles, final stock.
- **Headline:** Passed the squat test

### Ad 5: Swim, "Gold Coast day"

- **Headline:** Beach to lunch

### Ad 6: "How to get free shipping" (bigger orders)

- **Caption:** Free shipping on orders over $150. Grab a full set and it's on us. Plus 10% off your first order with BEBITA10.
- **Headline:** Free shipping over $150

### Ad 7: Talking to camera (trust)
- **Hook:** a model (or whoever's on camera) talking straight to the lens: "These are the last pieces of the original Bebita run."
- **Script (20s):** "When they're gone, they're gone, with no restocks. Every top and bottom is sold on its own so you can get your real size in each. Every order is packed and sent from the Gold Coast in 1–2 days."
