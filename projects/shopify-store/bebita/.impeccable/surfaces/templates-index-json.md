Scope: Bebita Lifestyle storefront home page (Shopify `templates/index.json` + custom sections), mobile-first, activewear line first. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: existing @bebitaactive / @bebitaswim followers plus new younger women who train, arriving on a phone from Instagram. Job: see the sets on real bodies, pick a colour and size, buy. Proof on hand: real model photography from the brand's Instagram; no reviews or press.
Constraints: Shopify-native cart and checkout; sell-off of remaining stock now, able to carry new drops later; honest about limited stock.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home page is the change-room mirror. Every set is shown the way the audience already shoots and reads it, a full-length mirror selfie with the phone in hand, refusing the category default of a studio model hero over a grid of product tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Mirror-glass frames with a thin chrome bevel and soft reflection on a warm blush ground; the brand's script "Bebita" mark; colourway pastels (baby pink, lilac, baby blue, cobalt, hot pink) carried by the photographs and by small colour chips, never as fills behind text. Phone-camera details as quiet UI: the time and battery in the corner of a mirror shot, a shutter-circle as the add-to-cart. Rounded only where a mirror or a phone is rounded.
STORY: The visitor recognises herself in the mirror shot, sees the set head to toe, picks her colour, and adds it to the cart before the sizes go.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait phone. Slim header with the script logo centred, menu left, bag right. One tall mirror-selfie frame fills most of the screen with a girl in a full set. Set name and price sit on the mirror's lower edge like a caption, a row of colour chips under it, and the add-to-cart shutter anchored at the bottom.
FORM: gym mirror selfie, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (the pick), seed 3f1c18a7. Raises: from the ikebana challenger, one photograph is allowed a whole screen of charged empty space; from the VU-meter challenger, colourways repeat as identical units so the row itself is the menu.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/home-comp-b-mirror-row.png (chosen by Hunter, 2026-09-30). The mirror photographs are placeholders for real Bebita photography; plates should come from the brand's own photos, not AI models.

Stated copy decision (Hunter, 2026-09-30): the positioning is "Build your set". The hero is built to the comp's words verbatim to pass the gate, then the headline changes from "Find your colour." to a Build-your-set line, and the page adds a build-your-set section (pick a top, pick a bottom, any size and colour) with a bundle price once the real prices are known.
