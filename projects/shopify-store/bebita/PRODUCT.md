## Platform

## Stack
Shopify Online Store 2.0, Horizon theme, edited remotely through the Admin GraphQL API with `STORE=bebita ../shopify.sh`. Custom work ships as theme sections and templates; native cart and checkout.

## Users
- Existing followers of @bebitaactive (~3.5k) and @bebitaswim (~7.1k) who already know the brand.
- New, younger customers who have never heard of Bebita. Hunter's words were "new people, kids etc"; whether that means younger women or children's sizes is **undecided**.
- Mostly Australian, shopping on a phone from Instagram.

## Product Purpose
Sell through Bebita's remaining activewear and swimwear stock now; if it sells well, relaunch the brand. Success now is clearing stock at a fair price; success later is a store that can carry new drops without a rebuild.

## Positioning
**Undecided.** The brand has real product and real content from its first run, but no confirmed claim beyond that. Don't invent one.

## Operating Context
- Two lines: Active (seamless scrunch shorts, sports bras, leggings in pastel pink, lilac, baby blue, cobalt, hot pink) and Swim (triangle bikinis, sold as separates, in red, pink, white, peach, cobalt, aqua, brown).
- Both Instagram accounts currently say "CLOSED DOWN" and ended on 50% off sales.
- Shipping from the Gold Coast: $9.95, free over $100, Express $14.95, Australia only.
- Returns: 30-day change of mind, unworn with tags; swimwear only with the hygiene liner intact; sale items exchange-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Undecided: product list, sizes, stock counts, costs and prices; whether the brand is presented as a sale or as a brand.
