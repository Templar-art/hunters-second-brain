# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Shopify Online Store 2.0, Horizon theme, edited remotely through the Admin GraphQL API (`themeFilesUpsert`) with [shopify.sh](shopify.sh). Custom work ships as theme sections and templates; Shopify's cart and checkout stay native.

## Users
Everyone who trains: from gym regulars buying their first pair of straps to lifters who already pull heavy. Mostly Australian, shopping on a phone, often from an Instagram or TikTok link.

## Product Purpose
Sell One Percent lifting straps direct to customers and build a training-gear brand that can add products later. Success is paid orders at a healthy margin, and a brand that reads as premium rather than as another AliExpress reseller.

## Positioning
**The build.** Silicone anti-slip grip along a 30cm strap, 5mm neoprene wrist padding, and a double D-ring cuff with hook and loop closure. The product is genuinely better made than the plain nylon straps it sits next to, and the site should prove it through detail, not adjectives.

## Operating Context
- One product today: Silicone Grip D-Ring Lifting Straps, one size, sold as a pair, A$39.95.
- Shipping from the Gold Coast via Australia Post: $9.95, free over $75, Express $14.95, Australia only.
- Owner-run: Hunter packs and posts orders himself.
- Store: One Percent Standard (`onepercentstandard.myshopify.com`), domain `onepercentstandard.com` planned.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must work inside Shopify: sections, blocks and templates editable in the theme editor, native cart and checkout.
- The range is one product now and must scale to several without a rebuild.
- Undecided: final product photography (AI shots planned from real samples), live social accounts, logo file.

## Brand Commitments
- Mark: "1%" in a circle with a purple arc; wordmark ONE PERCENT; tagline BETTER DAILY. Store name One Percent Standard.
- Colour black + purple #9B5DE5 (Pantone 2655 C). Montserrat, all caps (from Hunter's packaging blueprint).
- Voice: short, direct, gym-floor confident, no hype words.

## Product Principles
1. Show the build. Every claim is a visible detail: the silicone dots, the padding, the D-rings.
2. Premium through restraint, not volume. A small range done properly.
3. Honest pre-launch: no invented proof.
4. Mobile first; most visitors arrive from social.
