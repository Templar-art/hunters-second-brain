## About page

**Heading:** About One Percent Standard

One Percent Standard is a Gold Coast training brand built on a simple idea: the gains live in the last one percent. The final rep, the heavier pull, the set you nearly skipped.

We started with the piece of gear that ends more sets than anything else: grip. Our cuffed D-ring straps take your hands out of the equation so the muscle you're actually training does the work.

We keep the range small on purpose. Every product has to earn its place.

## FAQ

**What size are they?**
One size. The hook and loop cuff adjusts to fit most wrists.

**How do I put them on?**
Wrap the cuff around your wrist and fasten the hook and loop so it's snug. Thread the strap under the bar, wrap it around, and grip over the top. Twist to tighten.

**Which lifts are they for?**
Pulling movements where grip gives out first: deadlifts, rack pulls, rows, shrugs, and pulldowns. They're not for pressing movements.

**Are they sold as a pair?**
Yes, every order is a pair.

**How do I clean them?**
Hand wash in cold water with a mild soap and air dry. Don't put them in the dryer.

**How long does shipping take?**
We dispatch within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery is 2–6 business days, and Express is 1–3. Free Standard shipping on orders over $75.

**Can I return them?**
Yes. Unused items can be returned within 30 days for change of mind. Faulty items are covered under Australian Consumer Law. See our Refund Policy.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Not yet. Australia only for now.
