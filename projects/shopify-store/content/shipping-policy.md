# Shipping Policy

We ship from the Gold Coast, Queensland, with Australia Post. We currently ship within Australia only.

## Rates
| Service | Cost | Estimated delivery after dispatch |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $9.95 | 2–6 business days |
| Standard, orders $75 and over | Free | 2–6 business days |
| Express | $14.95 | 1–3 business days |

Delivery times are Australia Post estimates, not guarantees, and remote areas can take longer.

## Dispatch
Orders placed by 12pm AEST on a business day are usually packed and sent within 1–2 business days. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as your order ships.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If your tracking hasn't moved in 5 business days, or it shows delivered and you can't find it, email us at [EMAIL] with your order number and we'll chase it with Australia Post.

## Wrong address
Please check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it if the order hasn't shipped yet.
