Constraints: Shopify-native cart/checkout; sections editable in the theme editor; must scale to more products.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home page is the strap's engineering drawing. The build is argued with dimension lines, leader lines and part callouts, refusing the category default of a dark gym athlete hero over three icon boxes.
OWN-WORLD: A drafting sheet on black: #0B0B0C ground, faint graphite drafting grid, white linework, purple #9B5DE5 only on the dimension being read and the primary action. Montserrat caps: wide-tracked 11px labels against oversized heavy dimension numerals. A title block like a drawing sheet (DWG 1P-LS-BLK, REV A, SCALE 1:1). Square cells, no cards, no rounded corners, nothing boxed that a hairline could carry.
STORY: The visitor sees the strap drawn and measured, understands exactly what each part does, believes it is made properly, and adds a pair to the cart.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait phone. Hairline title-block header, 1% mark left, cart right. Oversized headline at the top. The strap laid flat and full-width at centre with three leader-line callouts (silicone grip, 5mm padding, double D-ring) and a purple 30CM dimension line spanning it. Price and a full-width purple ADD TO CART cell anchored at the bottom of the viewport.
FORM: engineering spec blueprint, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (the pick), seed 1df0f9fe. Raises: from the cracktro challenger, nothing is enclosed by a box where a baseline or hairline can carry it; from the agit-train challenger, one accent rationed to the single action it drives.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
