# Talking-Head Content System — 10x verticals

The volume engine for @10x.realestate + @10x.construction (and TikTok twins).
Talking heads are the one format that grows accounts without needing new client
footage — Hunter on camera, one take, phone or FX3, batch-shot.

## The shooting format (batch day)

- **Setup:** one location per batch (office, car, or on site). Vertical 9:16.
  Eye level, arm's length or small tripod. Natural light or the key you'd use
  on any interview. Lapel or phone mic close.
- **Length:** 30–45s. Hook inside the first 2 seconds — no intros, no
  "hey guys", no logo stingers. Cut on the last word.
- **Batching:** shoot 6 in one sitting (~45 min), alternate shirts/angles so
  they don't read as one session. That's 3 weeks of Tue/Fri talking heads
  per account from one batch.
- **Finish:** captions burned in (CapCut auto-captions, house style: white,
  no boxes), 10x overlay PNG optional on the outro frame
  (`10x Premium Kit/reels/` grammar). No music bed louder than voice.
- **Voice:** blokey, direct, zero jargon. Say it like you'd say it to a client
  in the ute. One idea per video.

## Script format

HOOK (first line, on-screen text too) → MEAT (3 beats max) → ENDER (one line,
soft CTA or punchline). Captions per vertical: 1–2 casual lines + 3 niche
hashtags. These are scripts as *beats*, not word-for-word reads — say it
natural, keep the hook verbatim.

## REAL ESTATE — 6 scripts

### RE1 · The twilight window
- **HOOK:** "You've got ten minutes a day to take the photo that sells this house."
- **MEAT:** Twilight window is ~10 min after sunset. Lights on, sky still blue, pool glowing. Every buyer scrolls past 40 daytime shots — the twilight one stops the thumb.
- **ENDER:** "That's why we schedule around it, not around lunch."
- **Caption:** The ten minute window we build every shoot around. #twilightphotography #realestatemarketing #goldcoastrealestate

### RE2 · Empty homes don't sell
- **HOOK:** "An empty living room photographs like a storage unit."
- **MEAT:** Buyers can't judge scale in an empty room. Virtual staging from the real frame — real light, real floor, furniture that fits. Costs a fraction of physical staging, done next day.
- **ENDER:** "Same room, one day later. You'd swear the couch was there."
- **Caption:** Empty to staged in a day. #virtualstaging #propertymarketing #realestateau

### RE3 · Why your listing needs a film, not a slideshow
- **HOOK:** "A slideshow of photos with music isn't a property video."
- **MEAT:** Movement sells space — a walkthrough shows flow, ceiling height, how rooms connect. 60 seconds, cut to the property's pace. Agents who run film get the listing AND the next one.
- **ENDER:** "The vendor watches it too. That's your next appraisal."
- **Caption:** Films win listings. #realestatevideo #listingagent #propertyfilm

### RE4 · The drone shot agents get wrong
- **HOOK:** "Most drone shots are taken too high to sell anything."
- **MEAT:** 400ft shows the suburb, not the property. The money altitude is low oblique — block, backyard, position on street, walkability. High wides are for context, one per set.
- **ENDER:** "Fly lower. Sell the block, not the postcode."
- **Caption:** Altitude is a decision. #droneRealEstate #aerialphotography #realestatetips

### RE5 · What buyers look at first
- **HOOK:** "Eye tracking says buyers look at photo one for three seconds. Photo two gets one."
- **MEAT:** Lead image = decision image. Twilight exterior or hero living space, never the bathroom. Order the set like a story: arrive, walk in, live there.
- **ENDER:** "If photo one doesn't stop them, photo twenty never loads."
- **Caption:** Your first photo is the whole game. #realestatephotography #listingtips #marketing

### RE6 · Shoot day walkthrough
- **HOOK:** "Here's everything that happens in a two hour listing shoot."
- **MEAT:** Walk the property once with the agent → stills room by room with the light, not against it → drone set → twilight if booked. Delivery next day, print + web + socials crops.
- **ENDER:** "Two hours on site. Lives online for the life of the listing."
- **Caption:** Start to finish on a listing shoot. #behindthescenes #realestatephotographer #processvideo

## CONSTRUCTION — 6 scripts

### CO1 · Document the build or it never happened
- **HOOK:** "Your best marketing asset gets demolished behind plasterboard every week."
- **MEAT:** Frame stage, services, slab pours — gone the moment they're covered. Monthly progress visits capture the story you can't reshoot. Handover film needs the journey, not just the reveal.
- **ENDER:** "You can't photograph last month."
- **Caption:** The build only happens once. #constructionmarketing #progressphotos #buildersau

### CO2 · Why builders lose Instagram
- **HOOK:** "Builders post the handover. Buyers follow the build."
- **MEAT:** The reveal is one post; the build is thirty. Progress content compounds — clients see momentum, future clients see proof of process. The feed that shows mess AND finish reads honest.
- **ENDER:** "The journey is the content. The handover is just the finale."
- **Caption:** Post the build, not just the bow. #constructionau #buildermarketing #sitecontent

### CO3 · The toolbox talk nobody films
- **HOOK:** "The most trustworthy thing on your site happens at 6:30am and nobody films it."
- **MEAT:** Toolbox talks, safety walks, apprentices learning — that's what clients mean by "good operator". Documentary frames, no acting, no hard hats posed at sunset.
- **ENDER:** "Real beats staged. Every time."
- **Caption:** 6:30am, no acting. #constructionlife #toolboxtalk #buildersofinstagram

### CO4 · Drone progress sets
- **HOOK:** "One drone visit a month replaces a thousand words with the client."
- **MEAT:** Same hover points every visit → time-lapse of the whole build. Clients share it, certifiers love it, disputes die when there's a dated aerial record.
- **ENDER:** "Same spot, every month. Watch the build stack up."
- **Caption:** Monthly aerials, same GPS point. #droneconstruction #timelapse #progressdocumentation

### CO5 · Fitout films win the next job
- **HOOK:** "The venue you just fitted out is your showroom. For about three weeks."
- **MEAT:** Fitouts photograph best between handover and opening chaos. One film session: details, joinery, lighting design. That film pitches your next three tenders.
- **ENDER:** "Shoot it before opening night. After that it's someone else's story."
- **Caption:** Handover week is the shoot window. #fitout #commercialconstruction #hospitalityfitout

### CO6 · What a progress film costs a builder
- **HOOK:** "A monthly progress film costs less than the site signage you already bought."
- **MEAT:** One visit, one edit, delivered vertical + wide. Use it for the client update, socials, and the tender pack. Signage advertises to a street; content advertises to everyone.
- **ENDER:** "The sign works one suburb. The film works everywhere."
- **Caption:** Cheaper than your fence banner. #constructionmarketing #progressfilm #buildercontent
