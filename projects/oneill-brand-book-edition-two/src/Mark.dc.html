The mark

Three lockups, no fourth:

Primary

Wordmark over PROPERTY. Default for boards, covers, stationery, the site header.
Minimum 40 mm / 150 px wide
Artwork to be supplied

Secondary

Wordmark alone. For tight horizontal bands: email footers, ad bars, vehicle livery, sponsor rows.
Minimum 28 mm / 110 px

Icon

The O and N monogram. Avatars, favicons, stamps, watermarks, or anywhere the full name already appears nearby.
Minimum 8 mm / 32 px

Clear space

cap height of the O
cap height of the O
The cap height of the O, on every side, on all three lockups. Nothing enters it: type, rules, a photo edge, another logo, a portal badge. On a vehicle or fascia, measure from the physical panel edge, not the artwork box.

Four colourways, chosen for contrast, never a fifth

Basalt on light — the default, any Stone, paper or pale ground
Stone on Basalt — dark grounds, night photography, footers
Basalt on Stone — print, stationery, card-weight pieces
Sage Grey on Basalt — the social avatar, where full Stone reads too stark
Over photography: use Stone, place it on the quietest part of the frame, or put it on a solid band beside the picture. Never a drop shadow.

Six things never to do to it

Don’t stretch it (scale both axes together), rotate it, recolour it (gold is the one colour this brand was built to avoid), add shadows or effects, fade it below full opacity (use the Sage colourway instead), or crop, box or draw a rule around it. Also: never sit Sage Grey type on a Sage Grey ground, the two greens measure 1.6:1 and the mark disappears.
Supplied as SVG. Always use the supplied artwork, unmodified.
