Photography

Natural light, honest colour, architectural framing. The property is the hero, if a viewer notices the photography before the house, the shoot has failed.

Do

Shoot early or late (long shadows and warm light suit the palette, midday sun blows out water).
Square the verticals.
Leave the room breathing (fewer props, nothing that won’t be there on inspection day).
Keep colour honest (grass is not emerald, the Broadwater is not turquoise).

Do not

Composite a sunset or sky that wasn’t there.
Run a filter, vignette or heavy graduated wash.
Set type across the middle of a photograph.
Use a stock image of a house that is not the house, ever.
Type on photography: where Stone type must sit on an image, measure the scrim against the actual pixels behind it, to 4.5:1. A flat sage wash cannot deliver that at any opacity. Where possible, prefer a solid Basalt, Sage Grey or Stone panel beside the picture over type fighting the picture.
