The brand

O’Neill Property is an independent Gold Coast agency built on the O’Neill name, not a franchise template with a local name added to it. The standard has to hold from the first appraisal to the last signature, because it is the family’s name on the board.

The promise

Listen first, give the straight version, and run the campaign properly to the end.

Who it is for

Owners and buyers on the Gold Coast, from a first apartment to a home on the water. The same standard at every price point.

What it is not

A franchise template, an exclusive boutique, or a brand that changes when someone edits someone else’s.
Flag: Edition One scoped this to “Northern Gold Coast” specifically. Confirm whether the service area is Northern Gold Coast or Gold Coast‑wide before this goes to print, this word choice runs through audience copy, SEO and imagery selection.

Brand personality, in eight words

Confident · Personal · Premium · Clear Knowledgeable · Warm · Results‑focused · Local

Never

Boastful · Over‑familiar · Pretentious · Abrupt · Jargon‑heavy · Fluffy · Salesy · Parochial

The nine rules, if nothing else is read

01
Say: “Property is personal.” Confident, warm, specific, never salesy.
02
Show: natural light. Architectural, uncluttered, honest colour.
03
Set in: Sinhala MN and Inter. Two families, never a third.
04
Build on: Sage Grey. Half of everything. Basalt for type, Stone to rest the eye.
05
Lead with: the O’Neill mark, always dominant, always with its clear space.
06
Campaign in: Own It, Preparing It, Sold It, Lease It, paired with the plain word wherever status must be instant.
07
Protect: whitespace. If it feels empty, it is probably right.
08
Never: gold, gradients or clichés. Premium comes from restraint, not decoration.
09
Test with: is the property the hero? If the answer is the agency, start again.
