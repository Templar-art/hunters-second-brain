Signage and print

Signboards

A board gets about two seconds of attention: one campaign word, one mark, one number.
One campaign word only, never two on the same board: Own It, Preparing It, Sold It or Lease It.
The mark sits on a Basalt headband, it reads further than a mark floating on sage and won’t disappear against a hedge.
The agent is the smallest element: name, then number, at the foot. A face is optional, a legible phone number is not.
Sage carries display type only, in Basalt (6.1:1). Nothing lighter than Basalt goes on sage.
One arrow points, it doesn’t decorate: a single chevron, on the side it points to, sized to match the campaign word.

Cards, flyers and stationery

The same system, smaller. Nothing new invented at A4 or card size.
Uncoated stock always: 350 gsm cards, 170 gsm flyers. Gloss undoes the chalky, natural palette.
Stone is the paper. Specify a warm uncoated stock rather than printing a near-white flat.
Request a rich black for any large Basalt area, a flat single black looks grey and patchy at size.
No foil, spot gloss or bevel. Lift a piece with paper weight or a Basalt edge paint, never shine.
One display size per piece: the campaign word largest, the address next, the agent smallest.
