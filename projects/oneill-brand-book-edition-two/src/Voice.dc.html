07 / 13

Voice

An agent speaking, not a brochure. Knowledgeable, warm, decisive, brief. Lead with what matters to the client, prefer a specific fact to an inflated adjective. Australian English throughout, no dash used as punctuation, no exclamation marks.

On brand

“North facing, 812 m², and the only home on the street with a private pontoon. We think it sells in three weeks.”

Off brand

“An absolutely breathtaking, once in a lifetime masterpiece that will take your breath away. A true must see!”

By situation

Price reduction
Direct and unapologetic.
Missed result
Honest, brief, forward-looking.
Market commentary
Plain, useful, unspun.
Complaint
Human first, process second.

Words to avoid on principle
