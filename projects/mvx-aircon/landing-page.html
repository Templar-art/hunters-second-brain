Quick answers

How fast can you get to me?

We're based on the Gold Coast and prioritise no-cooling breakdowns — most repair calls get a same-day response. Call 0427 593 990 and we'll give you an honest ETA on the spot.

How much does a split system install cost?

It depends on the unit size and the electrical work needed, but you'll always get a fixed upfront price before we start, including the dedicated circuit and compliance certificate. No surprises on the invoice.

Do I need an electrician for an aircon install?

Yes. Every install legally requires licensed electrical work for the circuit and isolator. That's the advantage of calling MVX: we're licensed electrical contractors AND ARC licensed, so nothing gets subcontracted.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

Rough rule: if the unit is 10+ years old and the repair is more than a third of a new system, replacement usually wins on running costs alone. We'll give you a straight recommendation either way, and we don't upsell.

Do you do commercial jobs?

Yes. Offices, shops, medical fitouts and facilities across the Gold Coast and Northern NSW, from single splits to multi-unit systems.
