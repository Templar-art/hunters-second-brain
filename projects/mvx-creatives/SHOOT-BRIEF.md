# MVX — Video Shoot Brief

**Monday 3 August** · Two ads · About an hour on site

---

## What we're making

Two Facebook ads. Both 20 seconds. Matt talking to camera, plus a few close-up shots of work happening over the top.

One ad is about **general electrical work** — the small jobs around the house people put off. The other is about **new builds and renos**.

We film each ad in three pieces: the opening line, the middle bit, and the sign-off. Then we cut them together. We film three different opening lines for each ad, because that's the part that decides whether anyone stops scrolling — so we try three and run with whichever one works.

---

## AD 1 — General electrical work

### Opening lines — pick one per cut, film all three

**1.**
> Everyone's got a list. Fan that doesn't work. Powerpoint hanging off the wall. Downlight that's been out since Christmas.

**2.**
> If you're getting a sparky out anyway, get the whole list done while he's there.

**3.**
> People call us for one thing. Then remember four more while we're standing there.

### Middle — same one every time

> We're MVX. Electricians, Gold Coast and Northern NSW.
>
> Fans, lights, powerpoints, switchboards, safety switches. All of it's us, so it all gets done in the one visit.
>
> And we'll tell you straight what needs doing and what doesn't.

### Sign-off — film both

**A.**
> Send us your list. We'll tell you what it takes.

**B.**
> Flick us a message with what's on the list, and we'll get back to you.

## Two lines to check before we film

- **"We turn up when we say we will."** Best line in either ad. Only works if it's true every time — happy to cut it if you'd rather not say it.
- **"Where the lights go, where the power goes."** Kept it simple on purpose. If you actually do proper lighting plans, say so and we'll change it.

---

## Saying it

- Talk to the camera like you're talking to a customer at the front door. Same voice.
- **Don't learn it word for word.** Read it a couple of times, then say it how you'd actually say it. If a word feels wrong in your mouth, change it — you'll sound better than the script does.
- Keep it short and stop. No need to fill the silence.
- Look at the lens, not the screen.
- We'll do each one three or four times. First one's always the worst, that's normal.
- If you fumble a word, keep going. Don't start again — we'll cut around it.

---

## What we film

**12 takes.** 3 openings + 1 middle + 2 sign-offs, twice over (once per ad).

**Two spots:**

1. **General electrical** — in front of the van, or at a switchboard. Somewhere that looks like a normal house.
2. **New builds** — an actual site. Frame up, bare studs, wires showing.

⚠️ **Need to know before Monday: have you got a site we can stand on?** If not, we'll do it with the plans on the ute tailgate instead — not as good, but it works. Better to know now than turn up and find out.

**Same shirt for both**, so we can mix takes between the two ads.

Filmed vertical, for phones.

### Close-ups we grab after

Quick ones, few seconds each, no fancy camera moves. Just hands doing the work.

**General electrical:** screwing a powerpoint cover on · fan starting up · opening the switchboard · testing a safety switch · putting a downlight in · shutting the van door with the tools.

**New builds:** cable running through the studs · marking a stud with a pencil · plans held up · drilling a joist · switchboard on a bare wall · wide shot of the frame with someone working.

We've already got a couple of these as photos, so we'll only shoot what's missing.
