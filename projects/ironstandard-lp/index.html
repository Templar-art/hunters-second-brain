What you're getting

Inside the facility

800m², purpose-built for serious training. 24/7 access, top-tier equipment, a dedicated posing room, plus on-site coaching, PT and physio. Location, opening date and pricing still being finalised, we'll share them here first.

Is this you?

Strength and physique athletes training seriously, competing or not, plus the coaches, PTs and physios who work with them. If you've ever fought for a rack, waited on a bench, or trained around a crowd instead of a plan, you already know what's missing. This is it.

Why it's different
  • Built for load: proper platforms and calibrated plates, not a token squat rack squeezed into a corner.
  • Coaching on-site: PTs and a physiotherapist in the same building, no running between three different addresses.
  • A membership base to match: everyone training to the same standard, so you're never the odd one out.
  • Run by the industry: founded by a competitive powerlifter, not an investor group chasing a franchise model.
Get in first

Get in early and you get in first. Everyone on this list gets founding-member benefits at launch: priority access and a discounted rate for a limited period. First come, first served, and total membership spots are capped, so the earlier you're on this list, the better your position.
