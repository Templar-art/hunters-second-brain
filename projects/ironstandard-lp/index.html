The facility

800m². Built to be used, not looked at.

Iron Standard is a premium strength and performance facility opening in Oslo: purpose-built for serious training, with 24/7 access, top-tier equipment, and a dedicated posing room. On-site coaching, personal trainers and physiotherapy are part of the same building, not an afterthought.

Exact location, opening date and membership pricing are still being finalised. We'll share them here first, before anywhere else.

Who it's for

For people who train seriously. Competing or not.

Strength and physique athletes, competitive and recreational, training several times a week. People who work with a coach or personal trainer, and the self-employed PTs and physiotherapists looking for a proper base to work from. If commercial gyms have felt crowded, under-equipped for heavy lifting, or restrictive about how you train, this is built for you instead.
