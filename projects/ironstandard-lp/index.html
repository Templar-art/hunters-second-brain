The facility

800m². Built to be used, not looked at.

Iron Standard is an 800m² purpose-built strength and performance facility opening in Oslo: proper platforms, calibrated plates, a dedicated posing room, and enough space to actually train the way you want to. Members get 24/7 access, not gym hours.

Who it's for

Serious lifters. Coached athletes. No exceptions made for either.

Strength and physique athletes, competitive and recreational, training several times a week. People who work with a coach or personal trainer, and the self-employed PTs and physiotherapists looking for a proper base to work from. If commercial gyms have felt crowded, under-equipped for heavy lifting, or restrictive about how you train, this is built for you instead.

What's different
