About Us

Built by someone who's lived the gap

Anthony McCoy spent ten years coaching athletes from their first session to national level, and competed as a national athlete himself, three times over. He came up through a Sports Management background before stepping inside SATS Fagerborg in Oslo, where the gap became impossible to ignore: one of the highest gym participation rates anywhere in the world, and almost nothing built for people who actually train hard. Iron Standard is what happens when someone who has coached that exact gap decides to close it.

Background

  • Ten years coaching athletes from beginner to national level.
  • Three-time national athlete, competing at the top of his own sport.
  • Sports Management background, before stepping into gym ownership.
  • Worked inside SATS Fagerborg, Oslo, and saw the gap first-hand.

Strategic advisor

Thomas Lilley is the founder of Zero Weakness, an established performance-gym operator with eleven locations across Australia, New Zealand and Europe. Iron Standard localises that proven model for Oslo.
