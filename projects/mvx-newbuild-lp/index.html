The kind of work we do

All of it planned ahead, not rushed. Here is what fits.

New home builds

We wire the whole house, from the first rough-in to the day you move in.

  • Full house wiring
  • Switchboard and safety switches
  • Lights, fans and power points
  • Fit-off and final connection

Renovations

Old wiring often will not handle a new kitchen or bathroom. We sort that out while the walls are open.

  • New circuits for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Moving or adding power points
  • Replacing old switches and lights
  • Rewiring rooms or a whole house

Extensions

Adding a room means adding load. We check the board can take it, then run the new work in.

  • Running power to the new part of the house
  • Checking the switchboard can handle it
  • Lighting and power point layout
  • Outdoor power and lights

Why it pays to ask early

Wiring goes in before the walls are closed up. Once they are lined and painted, changing it gets expensive.

  • 1

    You get the power points where you want them Think about where the TV goes, where you charge your phone, and which bench you use. We can put them there while it is easy.

  • 2

    Changes cost less before lining goes on Moving a light after the plaster is up means cutting, patching and painting. Before that, it is just a cable.

  • 3

    Your build stays on track Electrical has to happen at the right point in the job. Booked in early, it does not hold up the trades after us.

Questions people ask

Is this for emergency jobs?

No. This page is for work you are planning, like a build, a reno or an extension. If your power is out or something is unsafe, ring 0427 593 990 and we will help you from there.

When should I ask for a quote?

As early as you can. The best time is before the walls are lined, because that is when wiring is easy to change. Even if your build is months away, it is worth asking now.

What kind of projects fit?

New home builds, renovations, extensions, lighting changes, extra power points, and rewiring. Anything planned, big or small.

Can you help me work out where the lights and power points should go?

Yes. Most people are not sure, and that is normal. We walk through the rooms with you and work out what you need and where it should sit.

Do you work with builders?

Yes. We work directly with homeowners and alongside builders. If you already have a builder, we can talk to them about timing.
