Why it pays to ask early

Wiring goes in before the walls are closed up. Once they are lined and painted, changing it costs a lot more.

1

Power points end up where you want them

Think about where the TV goes, where you charge your phone, and which bench you actually use. We can put them there while it is easy.

2

Lights get planned room by room

How many downlights, where the switches sit, which lights you want on their own switch. Simple choices, much better to make them now.

3

You are not stuck with it later

Moving a light after the plaster is up means cutting, patching and painting. Before that, it is just a cable.

Questions people ask

Is this for emergency jobs?

No. This page is for work you are planning, like a build, a reno or an extension. If your power is out or something is unsafe, ring 0427 593 990 and we will help you from there.

When should I ask for a quote?

As early as you can. The best time is before the walls are lined, because that is when wiring is easy to change. Even if your build is months away, it is worth asking now.

What projects fit this page?

New home builds, renovations, extensions, rewiring, and adding lights or power points. Anything planned, big or small.

Can you help me work out where the lights and power points should go?

Yes. Most people are not sure, and that is normal. We walk through the rooms with you and work out what you need and where it should sit.

Do you work with builders?

Yes. We work straight with homeowners and alongside builders. If you already have a builder, we can talk to them about timing.
