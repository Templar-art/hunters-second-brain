# Huracán LP580-2 (ZEDDDD) in Blender

Hunter's reference photo: an orange Huracán LP580-2, VIC plate ZEDDDD, on a grass-paver drive (2026-10-21). He wants the car for a clip.

- `huracan_lp580-2.blend` (local only, `*.blend` is gitignored). The car is the `Sketchfab_model` hierarchy: 4.46 m long (real LP580-2 = 4.459 m), wheels on z=0.
- Paint: `baseM_CarPaint_Max1`, a deep orange with a clear coat (0.85/0.075/0 linear, metallic 0.25, coat roughness 0.02). Matched by eye to the photo under AgX Punchy.
- Plates front and rear: black face, thin white border, ZEDDDD in Arial Narrow Bold, small stacked VIC on the left (Hunter confirmed VIC). Objects are `plate*` and `plate*_rear`, parented to the car.
- Test renders: `test_v2.png`, `plate_closeup.png`, `plate_rear_closeup.png`.
