Option 01 · current, locked

Option 02 · proposal

Gallery Stone

Modern luxury at its quietest. Chalk, plaster and honed stone with very little timber, curved sculptural seating and solid stone tables. It reads like a private gallery or a design-hotel lobby. Best for new builds, white-walled apartments and architect homes with good light.

  • Four or six curved chalk bouclé chairs

  • Floating plaster-finish bedsides, both sides

Entry

Outdoor

  • Concrete-look low lounge, chalk weatherproof cushions
  • Round stone-composite dining table, curved resin chairs
  • Large textured planters with clipped olives

Styling kit

  • Large minimal abstract, chalk and warm grey, slim bronze frame
  • Stone and stoneware objects in groups of one or two
  • One sculptural branch per room, no leafy plants

Never

  • Cool grey or blue-white (it turns clinical)
  • Polished marble, gloss lacquer, chrome
  • More than two timber pieces per room
  • Busy patterned rugs or coloured cushions
Example prompt · living room · prose for Sunburst or Nano Banana Pro
A bright, calm modern luxury living room in soft natural daylight from the existing windows, keeping the exact camera position, focal length, framing and perspective of the original, identical composition and crop. The room contains exactly these pieces and nothing more: one low curved four-seat sofa in chalk-white boucle on the same footprint as the original seating zone, its back parallel to the [named wall] and facing the [named window or feature]; one solid honed travertine block coffee table with softened edges, centred on the sofa about 40 cm from the seat; one sculptural lounge chair in greige linen on a dark smoked-oak frame, angled toward the sofa; one slim brushed-bronze floor lamp beside the sofa's left arm; and a large pale stone-coloured flat-weave wool rug with its edges parallel to the walls, running under the sofa's front legs and the chair. The coffee table holds exactly two objects: one matte stoneware vessel holding a single olive branch and a stack of two neutral hardcover books. One large minimal abstract in chalk and warm-grey tones hangs centred and level above the sofa in a thin bronze frame. Full-height warm-white linen sheers hang at each window, each panel covering only its own frame width. Every piece stands square to the walls with real contact shadows. Natural true-to-life exposure with gentle directional shadows and subtle warmth. All existing taps, tapware, fixtures, windows, doors, vanities, cabinetry and tiles unchanged.

What this changes

Adds a curved chalk sofa, a stone block coffee table, one lounge chair, one lamp, a rug, two table objects, one artwork and sheers. Walls, floor, windows and camera stay as shot.

Pass confidence

70%

Risk: an all-pale room can read flat. If it does, the redo darkens the chair frame, not the walls.

Staging rules that do not change

  • Fixtures stay exactly as shot: tapware, joinery, benchtops, tiles, windows, doors, lights.
  • Camera framing is locked in every prompt. No re-crop, no new angle.
  • Each prompt lists the exact pieces in the room and the exact objects on each surface.
  • Hero pieces get three anchors: footprint, axis to a named wall, and a direction.
  • Furniture sits square to the walls; rug edges follow the walls and floor lines.
  • Both sides of every bed get a table and a lamp. Secondary bedrooms step down one tier.
  • Sheers on every bare window, each panel the width of its own frame.
  • TVs wall-mounted, screen side only. Mirrors show the staged room.
  • Soft natural daylight, true exposure. Final grade happens in Lightroom.
  • Every pass runs from the original photo, never from an AI output.
