## 1. "Is it a scam?" (their longest-running format, 2-3.5 min advertorial / 30-45 s video)

**Primary text**
Seen a lot of ads for these body-sculpting devices? Fair to ask if it's a scam. Here's the straight answer from the owner of Trivelle.

Cheap copies from marketplaces are a vibrating motor in a plastic shell. Trivelle has four modes (warm, red light, EMS micro-pulses, vacuum massage) and comes with the gel. It isn't magic, it isn't overnight, and results vary. Use it about 10 minutes, 3-5 times a week.

If you don't love it, you have 30 days to send it back for a refund.

**Headline:** Is it a scam? Honest answer inside
**Video hook (first 3 s):** on-screen "Is it a scam?" + presenter: "Fair question. I'd ask the same."
**Script beats:** (1) the question, (2) what cheap copies lack, (3) show the 4 modes, (4) honest limits ("not overnight, not for everyone"), (5) 30-day refund, (6) link.
