# Trivelle first-testers trial kit

Goal: a REAL trial. 20-30 women (35-65, Australia) get a Trivelle kit at cost, use it for 30 days, answer a short survey. We publish the real numbers with the sample size. Cost about A$45 landed per kit, so ~A$900-1,350 for 20-30 (check shipping with the supplier first).

Rules that keep it legal and credible
- Free product = disclose it. Every tester quote or photo used in ads says "received a free Trivelle for testing".
- Do not pay for, script or filter for positive reviews. Report all results, including "no difference".
- Get written consent before using a name, photo, video or quote.
- Cosmetic wording only in the survey and in the results ("looks", "feels"). No medical, fat or cellulite wording.
- Publish n every time: "X of Y testers". Never round a small n into a big claim.
- Testers can stop any time; anyone with skin sensitivity or a medical device (pacemaker etc.) or pregnant skips EMS modes and checks with their doctor first. Put this in the sign-up form.

## 2. Sign-up form (Google Form)
1. First name, email, mobile, state/postcode
2. Age bracket (under 35 / 35-44 / 45-54 / 55-65 / over 65)
3. Where will you mainly use it (face / neck / arms / stomach / thighs)
4. Current routine (none / creams / salon visits / other devices)
5. Can you use it 3-5 times a week for 30 days? (yes / no)
6. Safety (tick all that apply, any tick = we call you before sending): pregnant, pacemaker or implanted device, epilepsy, active skin condition in the area, recent surgery
7. Consent: I agree to answer a short survey, and that my answers may be published without my name. Photos or quotes only with a separate written yes.
8. Disclosure: I understand I receive the device at a testers' price and that honest feedback is what is wanted.
