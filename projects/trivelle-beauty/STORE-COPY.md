## How to use
1. **Apply the gel.** A thin, even layer on clean, dry skin.
2. **Glide slowly.** Choose a mode and start on the lowest level. Move in slow, upward strokes over each area for about 10 minutes.
3. **Wipe and moisturise.** Remove leftover gel, then follow with your usual skincare.

Use 3–5 times a week. Consistency is what makes the difference.

## FAQ

**How does it work?**
Trivelle combines four modes in one handheld device: gentle heat, red light, EMS micro-pulses and a vacuum massage. Together they give a salon-style body and face ritual you can do at home in about 10 minutes.

**What does it feel like?**
Warm and relaxing, with a light tingle on the EMS mode and a gentle pulling sensation from the vacuum. Always start on the lowest level and work up.

**Where can I use it?**
Stomach, thighs, arms, buttocks and jawline. Avoid the eyes, the neck's front (throat), and any broken or irritated skin.

**How often should I use it?**
About 10 minutes per area, 3–5 times a week. Results depend on consistency.

**Do I need the gel?**
Yes. The microcurrent gel helps the device glide and helps the EMS mode work evenly. An 80 g tube is included.

**Will it leave marks?**
The vacuum mode can cause temporary redness, like a massage. Start on the lowest level to avoid marks and keep the device moving.

**Who shouldn't use Trivelle?**
Don't use it if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have a pacemaker or other electronic or metal implant, have epilepsy, a heart condition, cancer, a blood clotting disorder, or on broken, sunburnt or irritated skin. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor first.

**How long does shipping take?**
Orders ship free with tracking and arrive in 7–15 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email.

**What if it's not for me?**
You have 30 days to try it. If you're not happy, contact us for a full refund. See our Refund Policy for details.

**How do I charge it?**
Use the included USB cable. A full charge lasts several sessions.
