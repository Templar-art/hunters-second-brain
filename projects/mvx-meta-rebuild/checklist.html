Turn off, rebuild, relaunch

Working checklist. The old campaigns can't be corrected because Meta locks the conversion location and performance goal once an ad set publishes, so they get paused and replaced. Work top to bottom.

Rule zero: don't go dark

Aircon is currently producing leads at about $27 each. If it's paused the same day the new campaign launches, there will be 3 to 7 days of no leads at all while Meta learns. Leave Aircon Push running until the new campaign has produced its first few leads, then pause it. Everything else in step 2 can happen today.

1. Before touching anything

  • Download the leads from both existing forms. Aircon Enquiry (11 leads) and General Electrician Enquiry (3). Do this first. Lead access can disappear once a form is archived, and this data is the only record of what the old settings actually produced.
  • Screenshot the 30-day stats for both campaigns: spend, leads, cost per lead. This is the baseline. Without it there's no way to prove the rebuild worked.

2. Turn off the old campaigns

Pause, never delete. Deleting destroys the account's conversion history and the only comparison point available.

  • Pause General Electrician | Lead Form | July 2026, today. 3 leads at $89.43 each over 30 days. Nothing is lost by stopping it.
  • Leave Aircon Push | Lead Form | July 2026 running for now. 10 leads at $27.28. It's the only thing producing and it's the control.
  • Pause Aircon Push once the new campaign has produced its first leads (expect day 3 to 7).
  • Leave both forms active until their leads are downloaded.

3. Build the three instant forms first

Build forms before ads: an ad can't be finished without a form to point at. Every form uses the same shape. The two settings that matter most are phone prefill OFF and the owner question first.

4. Build one ad set

One campaign, one ad set, three ads. Three separate campaigns at $15/day each would split the budget and the data three ways; one ad set lets Meta show which service it can actually sell, then that one gets scaled into its own campaign.

6. Test it yourself before it spends

  • Submit all three forms from your own phone. Two minutes. It also answers the "why is there no spam" question: Meta instant forms prefill from real profiles, so they rarely get any. Nothing was broken.
  • Confirm every submission arrives where Matt actually sees it. A lead that lands in an inbox nobody checks is the same as no lead.

7. Review at day 7

  • Kill any ad over $80 per lead.
  • Kill any ad producing only renters and "not sure" budgets.
  • Scale the survivor to $40 to $45 per day, which is where the Strategic Investing winner sits.
  • Judge on jobs quoted, not leads. Four good leads beat ten junk ones.
  • Compare lead quality against the old aircon leads. Same account, same audience, so any difference is the settings.
