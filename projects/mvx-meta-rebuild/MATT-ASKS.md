## Message 2 — call, or email if he prefers. This week.

Access and the decisions that unblock the campaigns. Worth a call rather than a text: three of these are questions he has to think about, not facts he can look up.

### The access ask — bundle it, don't drip it

Both of these need one sitting, and the form fix and the tracking install use the same WordPress session. Asking twice wastes his time and ours.

> Two bits of access so we can finish the setup properly:
>
> - **WordPress admin.** The enquiry forms on your two ad pages are asking for six things when they should ask four, and one of them still says "aircon" on the electricians page. Same login lets us put the tracking in.
> - **Facebook Business Manager, admin.** I've got enough to see the ads, not enough to set up the tracking side. It's a role bump, not new access.

⚠️ **Don't ask for DNS.** Domain verification can be done with a tag in the site header, which the WordPress access already covers. One less thing for him to chase.

### The three decisions

**1. The plumbing question — blocks the whole hot water campaign.**
> On hot water: are you subbing or partnering a plumber, or staying electrical-only? The water side of a changeover is licensed plumbing in QLD. Either way works, but it changes what we can say in the ad and I don't want to advertise something you can't deliver.

**2. Minimum job value worth your time.**
> Roughly what's the smallest job worth you turning up for? It sets what we filter for in the ads and what we cut.

Directly sets the budget bands in the instant form and the kill rules. Currently guessed.

**3. Which of the last leads he actually wanted.**
> Of the enquiries that came through the last month or so — which ones would you actually have wanted? Even just two or three of them.

This is the most valuable question on the list. Thirteen leads came through and roughly a third were out of area, but "quality" is still our inference rather than his definition. His answer is what makes *Maximise number of conversion leads* possible later, and it costs him two minutes.
