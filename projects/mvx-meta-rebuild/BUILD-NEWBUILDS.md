# MVX Meta — New Builds & Renovations

**Date:** 2026-07-28 · **Supersedes** the three-service plan in [BUILD.md](BUILD.md) and the checklist PDF.
**Offer:** electrical for new builds, renovations and extensions. Planned work only, not emergencies.
**Structure:** 1 campaign → 1 ad set → 1 ad → 1 form. Nothing more.

## Why this is a better offer than the old ones

The old campaigns sold symptoms: aircon not cooling, no hot water. Symptoms are emergencies, and emergencies go to Google, where people are already searching. Meta caught the cheap end of that: seniors chasing discounts, people wanting a $200 clean, one man who'd bought his own unit online.

New builds and renos are the opposite. Nobody Googles "electrician" the day they decide to renovate, but they do scroll Facebook for months while planning it. That is what Meta is actually good at, and the jobs are worth ten times a service call.

### The one setting I'd change manually, and why

Everything Advantage stays **on**, as briefed. It worked for Strategic Investing and it is the right call on a small budget: Meta needs room to find people.

But there is a separate dropdown underneath the location field, and it is not an Advantage setting. It offers:

- People **living in** this location
- People living in **or recently in** this location ← Meta's default
- People **travelling in** this location

On the default, the last aircon campaign produced leads from **Liverpool in Sydney and Canberra**, roughly a third of the total. Those aren't poor-quality leads, they're impossible ones. Setting it to "living in" costs nothing, doesn't fight the AI, and stops it.

1. Full name

**Thank you screen**
> **Thanks, we've got your enquiry.**
> We will review your project and be in touch about the next step.

Email stays in, unlike the callback forms on the landing pages. Different job: a quote for a build gets emailed, a broken hot water system gets a phone call.

No owner question. On this offer it's redundant, and Hunter's right that it was: someone planning a renovation is the owner.

## Budget and how long to leave it

**$20/day.** At Gold Coast CPMs that's enough for Meta to exit the learning phase in about a week, and it's roughly what the two old campaigns were spending between them anyway.

- **Days 1 to 3:** leave it completely alone. It will look bad. Cost per lead is meaningless this early.
- **Days 4 to 7:** still don't touch it. Meta needs about 50 conversions to optimise properly and it won't have them yet.
- **Day 7 to 10:** first real read. Judge on whether the leads are real projects, not on cost per lead.
- **Only then** change one thing at a time. Editing an ad set restarts learning and wastes the spend that got you there.

The most common way small budgets get wasted is being adjusted every second day.

## Judge it on this

Not cost per lead. A $90 lead that becomes a full house rough-in is a good day.

Ask instead: **are these real projects with real timelines?** Four enquiries from people actually building beats twenty from people curious about a light switch.
