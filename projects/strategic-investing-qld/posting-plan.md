## 05 · Why QLD · `out/post-03/post-03.png`

Why do so many investors keep looking at Queensland?

Because different QLD markets can offer different opportunities depending on your goals,
risk profile and timeline. The key isn't just buying in Queensland, it's choosing the
right strategy and the right area for your situation.

## 06 · Good questions · photo · `out/post-11/post-11.png`

The most useful strategy conversations start with simple questions.

Where are you now? Where do you want to be? And what's the plan in between? Clarity on
those three tends to shape everything else.

## 07 · Strategy Tip 01 · `out/post-08/post-08.png`

Before you start looking at suburbs, listings or "hot markets", get clear on the outcome
you actually want.

Are you chasing growth, cashflow, a long hold, or a balance of all three? When the
outcome is clear, the location decision gets much easier.

## 09 · Common mistakes · carousel · `out/post-04/` (slides 01 to 06)

A lot of investing mistakes happen before the property is even purchased.

The biggest issue is usually a lack of strategy. When you understand your goals, risk
tolerance and timeline first, you make far better decisions later.

## 10 · Time in the market · white · `out/post-22/post-22.png`

Timing the market perfectly is close to impossible.

Time in the market, holding a well chosen asset through the cycle, is what tends to do the
heavy lifting.

---

## 11 · Growth vs cashflow · `out/post-18/post-18.png`

Two words that shape most property strategies.

Growth is your value rising over time. Cashflow is the rent measured against your costs
today. Most plans use a blend, and the right mix depends on your goal.

---

## 12 · Different suburbs, different strategies · photo · `out/post-12/post-12.png`

Queensland isn't one market, it's many.

Growth, cashflow and timeline all point to different areas. The skill is matching the
suburb to your goal, not chasing the latest "hot" postcode.

---

## 13 · What happens in a strategy chat · `out/post-06/post-06.png`

A strategy chat is really just a structured conversation.

You look at where you're at now, get clear on your goals and timeline, talk through the
options, and outline some practical next steps. For a lot of people, that clarity is the
most useful part.

---

## 14 · Clarity is the whole strategy · white · `out/post-14/post-14.png`

Most investors don't need anything flashy. They need a plan that's clear enough to
actually follow. That's where good strategy starts.

---

## 15 · What a good strategy balances · `out/post-09/post-09.png`

Good strategy isn't about one number.

It balances four things at once: your goal, your cashflow, your timeline, and how much
movement you're comfortable with. Get those clear and the property decisions get a lot
easier.

---

## 16 · Know your numbers first · photo · `out/post-21/post-21.png`

Before you fall for a listing, get clear on your numbers: your borrowing power, the buffer
you want to keep, and the kind of yield you're aiming for.

The numbers build the shortlist, not the other way around.

---

## 17 · Strategy Tip 02 · `out/post-16/post-16.png`

A "good area" isn't automatically a good investment for you.

The right buy is the one that serves your strategy, your goals, your numbers, your
timeline, not just a postcode with momentum.

## 18 · Case study · carousel · `out/post-05/` (slides 01 to 04)

## 19 · Buy well · white · `out/post-23/post-23.png`

A lot of your return is set the day you buy.

Buying well, at the right price, in the right area, for your strategy, gives everything
that follows a head start.

---

## 20 · What is equity? · `out/post-19/post-19.png`

Equity is simply the part of your property you actually own: its value, minus what you
still owe.

It grows as you pay down the loan and as the property rises, and many investors use it to
help fund their next move.

---

## 21 · The right team · `out/post-07/post-07.png`

Property investing decisions don't happen in isolation.

Good strategy often involves the right conversations with the right professionals, across
strategy, finance, tax and property. The aim is more informed, coordinated decisions.

---

## 22 · Rentvesting, simply · `out/post-20/post-20.png`

Rentvesting means renting where you want to live while investing where the numbers work.

You keep the lifestyle and flexibility, and let your investment dollars go where growth or
yield stacks up.

---

## 23 · Strategy Tip 03 · `out/post-17/post-17.png`

Growth gets the attention, but cashflow is what lets you hold long enough to see it.

Build your buffers and plan your cashflow before you buy, not after.
