## 0. The one rule that decides everything

**Match the platform to how the buyer arrives.**

| Buyer state | Platform that works | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Something broke, needs it fixed today | **Google Search** | They are actively typing the problem. Demand already exists; you only have to be present. |
| Considering a big change, no deadline | **Meta** | Nobody searches "should I build a custom home". Demand has to be created and nurtured. |
| Knows the category, comparing suppliers | Google Search + retargeting | Intent exists but so do competitors. Differentiation and proof win. |

Every failure in the current three accounts is a violation of this: MVX has search demand for hot water and no page to catch it; CVA is trying to catch a 6–18 month decision with a "Contact us" form; 10x ran interruption-based Meta ads pointing at a site with no capture at all.

**Second rule, from the research:** service-specific landing pages convert at roughly **8–12%** where generic ones sit at **2–3%** ([mwolfmedia](https://mwolfmedia.com/google-ads-for-plumbers/)). One page per service, per intent. Never point a hot water ad at a general electrician page — that alone can cost three quarters of the leads.

**Third rule — the proven internal benchmark:** [[strategic-investing-qld]] produced **10–15 leads in 4 business days**. Confirm its attribution tomorrow (spend, objective, and *where the lead landed*) and copy that mechanic rather than inventing three new ones.

## 1. MVX Electrical — trades / emergency intent

### Why this niche works on Google, not Meta

Hot water and aircon are **breakdown purchases**. When the hot water goes cold, people don't scroll — they search, and they buy the same day. The research is unambiguous:

- Water heater keywords carry a **$400–$900 ticket with a high close rate** and produce consistent calls per dollar ([watsonco](https://watsonco.marketing/blog/2026/03/best-google-ads-keywords-plumbers-2026/)).
- **Emergency searches convert at 15–25%** when paired with a strong landing page and call-only ads in business hours ([mwolfmedia](https://mwolfmedia.com/google-ads-for-plumbers/)).
- Emergency repair calls book at ~**78%** vs ~**23%** for general consultation calls ([get-ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/ai-google-ads-for-plumbing-businesses-guide)).
- Cost: plumbing-adjacent CPCs run **$5–$30+**, CPL **$50–$100+**. Against a $400–900 job that clears easily.

**Implication for the pivot Matt wants:** hot water is *not* a harder sell than aircon — it's the same emergency mechanic with a comparable ticket. The only reason no hot water leads exist is that **no hot water page exists** (verified against MVX's full 29-page sitemap). Build the page, point search spend at it, and the leads behave like the aircon ones.

### ⚠️ Licence blocker — resolve before spending

MVX holds electrical / ARC / solar / cabler licences. **No plumbing licence.** In QLD the water side of a hot water swap is licensed plumbing work. Two honest routes:

| Route | The page promises | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Partner/sub a plumber** (recommended) | Full supply + install, end to end, one call | None, if the partner is locked in before ads run |
| **B — Electrical-only scope** | Heat pump changeovers, circuits, isolators, switchboard, compliance — "we do the electrical side, we coordinate the plumbing" | Weaker offer; some searchers want one supplier |

The landing page is written for **Route A** with the plumbing line clearly marked so it can be swapped to Route B in one edit. **Do not run traffic until Matt confirms which.**

### The offer that does the work

Heat pumps attract federal **STCs worth ~$998**, normally applied as an upfront discount: heat pump ~$2,600–6,300 installed → **from ~$1,600** ([Modern Hot Water](https://www.modernhotwater.com.au/guides/heat-pump-hot-water-rebates-queensland-gold-coast-homeowners), [Greater Plumbing](https://greaterplumbingco.com.au/hot-water-gold-coast/guides/hot-water-rebates-qld-2026)). That is a genuine, verifiable, number-led hook — and heat pump work is electrician-led, which makes MVX the *right* trade rather than a stretch.

⚠️ Confirm current STC pricing with Matt's supplier before the page goes live. Rebate values move, and a stale number on a landing page is a compliance problem, not a typo.

## 2. CVA Architects — long-consideration professional services

### Why the current setup can't produce leads

Every CTA on cvaarchitects.com.au goes to `/contact`. A contact form catches only people who have **already decided to hire an architect** — the last 5% of the market. Architecture carries a 6–18 month consideration window, so the other 95% needs a reason to raise their hand *before* they're ready to commission.

Research backing the approach:
- Construction-sector CPLs run **$66–$80** ([leadgulls](https://leadgulls.com/academic-articles/digital-marketing-architectural-firms-google-ads-facebook-ads-2026)) — expensive per lead, but against an architectural fee that's trivial.
- **Qualifying copy beats volume copy.** One firm dropped price language entirely for scope/process/specialisation wording plus "project minimums apply" — lead volume fell **22%** while Google Ads revenue *rose* within two months ([bgcollective](https://www.bgcollective.com/solutions-lab/the-2026-google-ads-playbook-for-remodelers)).
- Judge on **cost per booked project**, not CPL. The highest-CPL accounts in this sector are often the most profitable because they qualify hard.

**So: CVA's ads should deliberately repel price shoppers.** Fewer, better leads. This is the opposite instruction to MVX, and getting it backwards is how architects end up quoting tyre-kickers all month.

### The offer: a paid-thinking-shaped free step

Not a PDF nobody reads. The offer that works for architects is **"can I actually build what I'm imagining, on my block, for my budget?"** — the question every prospect is stuck on.

**"Site & Feasibility Chat"** — a 30-minute conversation, no charge:
1. What the site allows (council overlays, setbacks, slope, orientation)
2. Whether the brief is realistic against the budget
3. What the process and rough fee range look like

Cheap for CVA (Cameron is doing this on calls already), and it self-qualifies: someone who owns a block and books a feasibility chat is a real prospect. Add **"our projects typically start from $X in build cost"** on the page to filter early — Cameron sets that number.

### Campaign structure

**Google Search** (small budget, high intent):

| Ad group | Keywords |
|---|---|
| Architect local | `architect gold coast`, `architects gold coast`, `residential architect gold coast` |
| Custom home | `custom home design gold coast`, `custom home architect`, `architect for new build` |
| Renovation | `renovation architect gold coast`, `home extension architect`, `knockdown rebuild design` |
| Development | `townhouse design architect`, `small development architect gold coast`, `duplex design gold coast` |

Negatives: `jobs`, `salary`, `course`, `degree`, `internship`, `software`, `free plans`, `drafting cheap`, `3d render` (they supply their own renders).

**Meta** (the volume half — creating demand):
- Objective: Leads (instant form) or Traffic to the feasibility page, tested against each other
- Audiences: GC homeowners 35–65 in the prestige/renovation suburbs; interests around architecture, home design, renovation; **plus a lookalike from past enquiries** if CVA has a list
- Retargeting: everyone who viewed the feasibility page but didn't book — this is where a long consideration window actually gets monetised
- **Creative is CVA's unfair advantage and it's already paid for:** the June job produced **19 stills + ~40 clips** that nobody is running. Get Cameron's OK and put them to work.

### Meta primary text — three angles to test

**A (feasibility / the stuck question):**
> Not sure whether the block will take what you've got in mind? That's the question we answer first. A 30 minute site and feasibility chat with a Gold Coast architect: what the site allows, whether the brief fits the budget, and what the process actually looks like. No cost, no obligation.

**B (process / anti-anxiety):**
> Most people don't hesitate on design. They hesitate because they don't know what happens after they say yes. Here's the whole path: brief, concept, approvals, construction. Architect-led from first sketch to site. Book a 30 minute feasibility chat.

**C (qualifying / repels the wrong lead):**
> We design a small number of homes and boutique developments each year on the Gold Coast, and we're upfront that architect-led work carries project minimums. If you're planning a considered build rather than the cheapest set of plans, start with a 30 minute feasibility chat.

### Google RSA copy — CVA

**Headlines:** Gold Coast Architects · Architect Led Home Design · Custom Homes & Developments · Free 30 Min Feasibility Chat · Design Shaped By Site & Brief · Concept To Approvals To Site · Boutique Gold Coast Practice · Will Your Block Allow It? · Renovations & New Builds · Talk To The Architect Direct

**Descriptions:**
1. Architect-led design for custom homes, renovations and boutique developments on the Gold Coast.
2. Start with a 30 minute feasibility chat: what your site allows, and whether the brief fits the budget.
3. No fixed house style. Every project starts with your brief, your site and your budget.
4. Considered, architect-led work. Project minimums apply. Book a feasibility chat to find out more.

## 3. 10x Visuals — B2B creative services

### Why there are currently zero leads

Three compounding problems, all structural:

1. **No lead capture exists.** 10xvisuals.com.au has no form — contact is a `tyson@` mailto plus a phone number. A Meta campaign pointing here cannot produce leads no matter how good the creative is. This is almost certainly why the 20 Jul campaign spent ~$160 for zero ([fb-ads-handoff](../fb-ads-handoff.md)).
2. **No offer.** "Contact us" asks a stranger to start a sales conversation. Nobody volunteers for that.
3. **Credibility gap.** "Lead Gen" sits in the nav as a service, sold from a site that captures no leads.

### What works in this niche

- **Creative volume is the lever.** Advertisers rotating **30+ creatives per campaign see up to 43% lower cost per lead** than those refreshing monthly (Meta benchmark data, via [gohighlevel](https://app.gohighlevel.com/v2/preview/I0tBqX4Y5ZF7ZSphpAxz/post/ai-video-ads-b2b-lead-generation-1238)). **10x is a video production company** — the thing that makes ads cheap is the thing 10x sells. This is the single biggest unfair advantage in the whole account book and it is currently unused.
- **52% of realtors name social as their top lead-gen channel**, and while ~40% of buyers use video in their search, very few listings feature it ([neilpatel](https://neilpatel.com/blog/real-estate-video-ads/)) — a gap 10x can name explicitly in the ad.
- AU real-estate market benchmarks from the earlier verified research: **CPM ~AU$30**, link **CTR ~1.68%**. At $40/day that's roughly 1,300 impressions/day, so expect a working setup to yield about **1 lead per 1–1.5 days** — not ten. Set expectations accordingly, and don't judge a campaign dead in 48 hours.

### The offer

Stop selling "video production". Sell a **named, finite thing with an obvious outcome** — and lead with proof, since 10x's product is visual:

**Primary: "Listing Film Test Drive"** — one property, one film, fixed price, delivered in 72 hours. Turns a retainer decision into a low-risk trial. Agents can say yes to it without a meeting.

**Secondary (top of funnel): "The Listing Video Teardown"** — send an agent a 3-minute Loom pulling apart their last listing video (what the first frame does, where the drone hurts them, what the edit buries). Costs 10 minutes, lands as expertise not a pitch, and it's genuinely useful even if they never buy.

Both are far stronger than the current implicit offer, which is "look at our portfolio, then email Tyson".

### Campaign structure

**Meta — the main channel** (this is a demand-creation product):
- Objective: **Leads with an on-Facebook instant form.** Not Traffic. The 20 Jul campaign's likely #1 fault.
- Form: name, phone, agency name. Three questions, nothing more.
- Audience: Gold Coast + SEQ, job titles/interests around real estate sales, property development, building; lookalikes from past client lists; broad Advantage+ given post-Andromeda ranking favours creative variety over narrow targeting.
- **Creative: 8–10 variants at launch, rotating monthly.** Actual listing films as the ad, not graphics about listing films. Show the product working.
- Retarget everyone who watched ≥50% but didn't submit.

**Google Search — small and surgical:** `real estate videographer gold coast`, `property photography gold coast`, `real estate video production`, `drone photography real estate gold coast`, `construction progress video`. Low volume, high intent, cheap. Don't expect scale here.

**Note on the dead campaign:** "investment leads" as a concept was never clearly defined. Kill it and relaunch against the Listing Film Test Drive, and collect the 8 diagnostic data points from [fb-ads-handoff.md](../fb-ads-handoff.md) first so the post-mortem is real rather than assumed.

### Meta primary text — three angles

**A (the gap, named):**
> Nearly 40% of buyers use video when they search for a home. Almost none of your listings have one. We shoot listing films for Gold Coast agents: one property, one film, delivered in 72 hours, fixed price. Watch one, then decide.

**B (test drive / low risk):**
> You shouldn't have to sign a retainer to find out whether a videographer is any good. So: pick one listing, we'll film it, fixed price, delivered in 72 hours. If it doesn't outperform your usual photos, don't book us again.

**C (teardown / expertise-first):**
> Send us your last listing video and we'll send back a 3 minute teardown: what the opening frame is doing, where the drone shot is hurting you, and what your edit is burying. Free, no pitch, genuinely useful.

**Headlines:** Listing Films In 72 Hours · One Property, One Film, Fixed Price · Gold Coast Property Films · See It Before You Commit
