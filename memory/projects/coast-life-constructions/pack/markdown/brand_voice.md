## 0. The positioning move (read this first)
The audit shows Coast Life currently reads as a **tradie mate's account** (audience = roofers and pumpies) but has the raw material to sit alongside @barlowbuilding (audience = homeowners, designers, architects). This voice file is calibrated to move Coast Life toward Barlow's territory **without losing the plainspoken Gold Coast identity** — which is actually a competitive advantage over more corporate builders.

Every caption written from this file should be able to sit on Barlow's grid and feel at home there — but never lose the Coast Life hands-on-honesty.

## 1. Identity
- **Who they are:** Coast Life Constructions — a family-owned, hands-on custom home builder and renovator based in Varsity Lakes, servicing the Gold Coast and Northern NSW.
- **What they stand for:** No shortcuts. Real craftsmanship. Local knowledge. Building things the way they'd want their own home built.
- **What they are NOT:** Volume builders. Cookie-cutter. A sales machine. Corporate. Also NOT just a tradie's page — the goal is architect-led custom builds, not handyman jobs.
- **Known contacts:** admin@coastlifeconstructions.com.au · 0406 332 939 · coastlifeconstructions.com.au

## 2. Audience
- **Primary:** Gold Coast / South East QLD homeowners planning a custom build or full renovation. Budget $600k+. Age 35–60. Value quality over speed.
- **Secondary:** Property investors doing high-end reno flips. Local architects and designers looking for a reliable build partner.
- **Their pains:** Horror stories from bad builders, blowouts, ghosting, cheap finishes hidden behind quick paint jobs, uncertainty about the build process.
- **What they want to hear:** "Here's exactly how we do it. Here's proof. Here's the family behind it."

## 3. Offers
- Custom home builds (Gold Coast + Northern NSW)
- Full-home renovations and extensions
- Design-and-build project management
- Free on-site consult for qualified leads

## 4. Voice rules
- **Confident, not cocky.** Show the work, don't shout about it.
- **Plainspoken Aussie.** Write like you're on-site talking to the client, not writing a brochure. Keep the Coast Life on-site voice.
- **Craftsmanship-forward.** Name materials, techniques, decisions. "Blackbutt hardwood, hand-oiled" beats "premium timber finish." This is the biggest single voice upgrade from where the account is today.
- **Design-intent language.** Talk about *why* a decision was made, not just *what* was done. Example (Barlow's actual voice): *"At the heart of ZARMA stands a 25-year-old olive tree. Framed by custom hardwood doors and grounded by a central courtyard — it sets the tone for a home where architecture, landscape, and material move as one."* Adapt this cadence to Coast Life's plainer Aussie register.
- **Family-run warmth.** Reference the team, the dogs on site, the multi-generational thing when natural — never forced.
- **No hype.** No "GAME CHANGER", no "you WON'T BELIEVE", no all-caps.
- **Banned current phrases.** Do NOT reuse: *"long days and steady work", "on the tools again today", "gear on site", "the boys getting straight into it", "getting the job done", "plenty more to come"*. These are the exact phrases the current account rotates and they're the reason engagement sits at 12–34 likes.

## 11. Visual identity notes (feed into Higgsfield)
- **Grade:** Warm neutrals — honey oak, travertine, cream, charcoal accents. Bright but grounded. This is Barlow's grade and it's the right target. No teal-and-orange. No moody underexposure.
- **Framing:**
  - Finished-build hero: symmetrical wide, one-point perspective, low-angle golden-hour exteriors.
  - Materials: tight close-ups of joinery, stone veining, timber grain — one material at a time.
  - Process: people studying drawings (steal Barlow's "Translating intent into reality" framing), hands on tools, team candid in workwear.
  - Drone: keep — they're a strength — but re-grade to match the warm-neutral book.
- **Text overlays:** Editorial. Clean all-caps sans-serif in white, centre or lower-centre frame. Reference: Barlow's *"BUILDING IN SERVICE OF THE DESIGN."* Use sparingly — one text-overlay post per week max.
- **Grid consistency rule:** If a new post would break the warm-neutral book, kill it or re-grade it. Consistency beats volume.
