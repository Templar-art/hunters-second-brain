## Strategy — five moves
1. **Get credited, not just tagged.** Make "credit @10xvisuals.co in the caption credit block" a standard deliverable line for every client (CLC launch calendar already does this). Every client post crediting 10x = a referral pipe to the profile. This is THE growth channel for a B2B media co — Grimmond→JUNE proves it.
2. **Name the work.** Every shoot gets its project name in captions + a story highlight per client/project (SŌL, ACC · Broncos Club, CVA, Maker, Zed, CLC…). 10x already named SŌL once; systematise it. Highlight covers from the editorial system.
3. **One design system.** The build_all.py editorial system (photo-first, light scrim, Poppins/Montserrat, no dashes) is the ONLY graphics language on the grid from now on. Retire the serif-script and sideways-type one-offs.
4. **Bio upgrade (B2B positioning):**
   > 10X VISUALS.
   > Film · Photo · Marketing for property & construction brands
   > Gold Coast · Latest work ↓
   (drops emoji pipes; speaks to agents/builders/developers, not consumers)
5. **Pillar mix (media-co version):** 40% client-work reveals (always collab-posted) · 25% BTS craft (organic-showcase voice) · 20% results/proof (sold-in-X-days, views, case numbers — verified only) · 15% people (faces on camera — the Grimmond founder lesson applies to Hunter/Kris/team).
