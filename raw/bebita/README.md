# Bebita raw material

- `instagram-active/`: 271 images saved from @bebitaactive on 2026-09-30, one per post. Carousels contribute their first slide; reels contribute their cover. Images are 1350px wide JPEGs straight from Instagram's CDN.
- `instagram-swim/`: 518 images saved from @bebitaswim the same way. Filenames carry `reel_` for reel covers.
- Filenames follow `<account>_<order>_<shortcode>.jpg`, where order 001 is the newest post. The shortcode opens the post at `instagram.com/p/<shortcode>`.

These folders are git-ignored (hundreds of MB). They are the brand's own photography and the stand-in until Hunter's sister supplies the original files.
