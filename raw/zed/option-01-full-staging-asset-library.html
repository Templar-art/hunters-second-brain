Text-only assets Claude must add

Some finishing items are better controlled by short prompt blocks than by product images. Add them only after the major furniture reads correctly.

OPTION 01 SOFT FURNISHINGS Add an oversized ivory wool or handwoven rug with subtle texture and no bold pattern. Use warm ivory linen curtains or full-height sheer drapery where physically possible. Add only two to four muted eucalyptus-olive cushions, one oatmeal throw and no decorative clutter. OPTION 01 ART AND STYLING Add one oversized tonal abstract artwork in a thin oak frame: soft taupe, putty, limestone, muted olive and charcoal. Use one textured ceramic vessel with olive branches, two neutral hardcover books and one small honed-stone bowl. Keep all surfaces mostly clear. OPTION 01 LIGHTING Use a large sculptural linen or paper pendant over dining. Use ceramic lamps beside beds. Use an aged-brass dome lamp in an office, entry or living room as a restrained accent. Keep lighting warm but not orange. OPTION 01 OUTDOOR FINISHING Use large matte stoneware or limestone planters with olive trees or mature subtropical greenery. Add warm-ivory outdoor cushions, maximum two muted-olive accent cushions, a low teak or limestone coffee table and a natural outdoor woven rug only where appropriate.

Universal prompt block

Use the uploaded property image as the structural reference. Use the client style image for the approved Option 01 look. Use the selected product images only for individual furniture silhouettes, construction and materials. Style: warm organic contemporary with restrained Australian coastal and Mediterranean-resort influences. Warm ivory linen, oatmeal bouclé, dark smoked walnut, honed travertine, warm limestone, muted eucalyptus olive and subtle aged brass. Low-profile sculptural furniture, minimal styling, soft natural daylight, open shadows, controlled highlights, low saturation and premium Australian architectural photography. Do not use Hamptons, blue coastal, whitewashed timber, farmhouse details, black marble, glossy gold, bright green, orange timber, decorative clutter or artificial golden-hour light. Prioritise physically plausible furniture scale, clear circulation, correct verticals, real floor contact shadows and one consistent natural window-light direction. Final campaign colour will be completed in Lightroom.
